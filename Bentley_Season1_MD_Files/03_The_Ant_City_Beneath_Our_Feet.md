# The Ant City Beneath Our Feet
### Season 1 · Episode 3 · Bentley's Discovery Club

> **Logline:** Bentley shrinks to ant-size for the first time and meets Dot, a worker ant who feels like a nobody — "just one tiny ant who doesn't matter" — right as a rainstorm threatens to flood the entire underground city.

**Habitat:** An ant megacity under an Orange County backyard · **Power used:** Listening + **shrinking** (debut!) · **Guest:** **Dot**, a worker ant.
**The kid-feeling we're really telling (SEL):** *Feeling small and unimportant — and learning that the many need the one.*
**Bentley's arc:** Shrinks and immediately tries to *boss the ants her way* (doing) → breaks their system → learns to follow *their* ancient method → listens to Dot.

## The 3 Dramatized Facts (real)
1. An ant colony is a **superorganism** — one ant is nearly helpless, but together they farm, build, and out-engineer almost anything. The colony "thinks" as a group.
2. Ants **talk by smell** — they lay **pheromone trails** so the whole colony can follow the path to food or safety.
3. Ants can carry **many times their own body weight**, and they link bodies to build **living bridges and rafts** (some float a whole colony on water!).

## Cold Hook (0:00–0:12)
The shell flares — the world EXPLODES upward. A pebble becomes a boulder; a blade of grass becomes a redwood; a single ant looms over Bentley like a bus. **BENTLEY:** "Why is everything getting so... *BIG?!*"

## The Bentley Method
- **Flaw on display:** Tiny Bentley appoints herself "Captain" and tries to lead the ants on a faster shortcut. They ignore her and follow an invisible path. She "corrects" them — and scuffs out their scent trail. Chaos. (Comedy: she keeps yelling "FOLLOW ME!" to ants who absolutely will not.)
- **Discovery + it gets worse:** Storm clouds gather. With the trail broken, foragers are lost and the egg chamber is in the flood path. Bentley's "help" stranded half the colony.
- **THE TURN:** Stop. Look. Listen. Shell glows. Bentley gets low, *smells* the faint trail like a dog should, and hears Dot: *"I'm just one ant. I can't fix this. I never matter."*
- **Climax — clock + setback:** Rain hits; tunnels start flooding. Bentley tries to dam it solo — fails (one dog, one paw). Setback → she rallies the colony to do what ants *really* do: link legs into a **living bridge/raft** to ferry the eggs to high ground while Bentley re-lays the scent path with a dab of nectar. Dot leads the last rescue.
- **Emotional button:** "You're not just one ant. You're one of a *million* — and the million needed the one. Tonight, the one was *you.*"

## Signature Scene (dialogue)
> **DOT:** "There are a million of us. Nobody would even notice if I was gone."
> **BENTLEY:** "Okay but — who found the broken trail first?"
> **DOT:** "...I did."
> **BENTLEY:** "And who's gonna lead everyone across that bridge?"
> **DOT:** *(small, then growing)* "...I am."
> **BENTLEY:** "See? Small isn't the same as 'doesn't matter.' Trust me, I'm currently the size of a *crumb.*"

## Cliffhanger → Episode 4
The rain clears. A single brilliant **monarch butterfly** lands beside tiny Bentley, wings trembling. *"I'm leaving today. On the Big Journey. And I'm... I'm not ready."* The shell glows toward the sky. → *The Butterfly Highway.*
