# The Butterfly Highway
### Season 1 · Episode 4 · Bentley's Discovery Club

> **Logline:** Bentley joins the great monarch migration alongside Milo, a young butterfly terrified to leave home because he doesn't know the way — and learns that no single butterfly *ever* knows the whole journey.

**Habitat:** The sky — the monarch migration route down the California coast · **Power used:** Listening + **travel with migrators** · **Guest:** **Milo**, a first-time migrating monarch.
**The kid-feeling we're really telling (SEL):** *Fear of a big change / a big first step when you can't see the whole path — first day of school, moving, growing up.*
**Bentley's arc:** Tries to *push and lead* the scared butterfly fast (doing) → exhausts him, they get lost → learns the migration works by *following others, one flower at a time.*

## The 3 Dramatized Facts (real)
1. Monarchs migrate **thousands of miles** to overwinter; California monarchs gather by the thousands in coastal groves (like Pacific Grove's eucalyptus).
2. **No single butterfly knows the whole route** — the full migration takes **several generations.** The ones who finish are the great-grandchildren of the ones who started. They navigate by the sun.
3. Butterflies **taste with their feet**, and each one began as a caterpillar that rebuilt itself completely inside a chrysalis (**metamorphosis**).

## Cold Hook (0:00–0:12)
The sky turns to fire — an ocean of orange wings pouring overhead. **BENTLEY:** *(spinning, mouth open)* "It's... it's *RAINING butterflies!*" One small flutter lands on her nose, shaking. *"I can't do it. It's too far."*

## The Bentley Method
- **Flaw on display:** Bentley grabs Milo and tries to *fly him there fast*, flapping ahead, yelling "Keep UP!" She tires him out and they peel off the main river of butterflies — lost over open water.
- **Discovery + it gets worse:** A cold wind rises. Butterflies can't fly when they're cold; Milo's wings stiffen. Bentley's rush put them in danger.
- **THE TURN:** Stop. Look. Listen. Shell glows. Bentley hears Milo's real fear: *"I thought I had to know the WHOLE way. I thought I had to do it alone. And I don't even know where 'there' is."*
- **Climax — clock + setback:** They must reach the grove before nightfall's cold. Bentley's "I'll carry you the whole way" fails — she's not built to fly that far either. Setback → they rejoin the great cluster, warm up wing-to-wing, and ride the right air current *together.* Milo only ever flies to *the next flower.*
- **Emotional button:** "You don't have to see the whole journey. Just the next flower. And look around, Milo — you are *never* flying alone."

## Signature Scene (dialogue)
> **MILO:** "But what if I get it wrong? What if I don't make it?"
> **BENTLEY:** "Did you know not *one* butterfly here has ever seen the end? It takes your kids, and your kids' kids, to finish. They're all just flying to the next flower they can see."
> **MILO:** "...That's it? Just the next flower?"
> **BENTLEY:** "Just the next flower. And then you look up, and you're already halfway to somewhere amazing."
