# Bentley's Discovery Club
# Pilot Episode 0: Bentley's Big Discovery

## Episode Purpose

This pilot introduces Bentley, her personality, Orange County, California, and the magical premise of the series.

**Primary Goal:** Make the audience fall in love with Bentley.

**Runtime:** 8–10 minutes

---

# Cold Open

Sunrise over Orange County.

Golden light spills across the beach.

Waves roll onto the sand.

Seagulls soar overhead.

Bentley, a young female Boxer dog, races across the beach carrying a stick twice her size.

Narrator:

"Meet Bentley.

Bentley loves beaches.

Bentley loves adventures.

Bentley loves snacks.

But most of all... Bentley loves questions."

Bentley drops the stick.

"Why do waves never get tired?"

---

# Character Introduction Montage

Bentley explores her world.

Questions appear rapidly:

- Why do birds fly in a V?
- Why is the ocean salty?
- Why do crabs walk sideways?
- Why are ladybugs red?

Bentley investigates everything.

She gets distracted often.

She always tries to help.

---

# Inciting Incident

Bentley discovers a strange glowing shell.

The shell whispers:

"Help..."

Bentley looks around.

Nobody is there.

The shell glows brighter.

A tiny voice says:

"Down here!"

---

# Adventure Begins

The shell leads Bentley to a hidden tide pool.

The water sparkles.

Tiny sea creatures suddenly speak.

A sea star stretches.

A hermit crab waves.

An anemone yawns.

Bentley gasps.

"You can TALK?"

---

# Science Discovery Section

Bentley learns:

- Tide pools are ocean habitats left behind when tides go out.
- Sea stars can regrow lost arms.
- Hermit crabs use abandoned shells as homes.
- Sea anemones are animals, not plants.

Every fact is revealed naturally through character dialogue.

---

# Emotional Moment

Bentley asks:

"Why can I understand you?"

Stella the Sea Star smiles.

"You always could.

You finally started listening."

---

# Climax

A baby hermit crab has lost its shell.

Bentley helps organize a shell search.

The tide pool community works together.

The baby crab finds a new home.

---

# Ending Lesson

Narrator:

"The world is full of secrets.

And every secret begins with a question."

Bentley smiles.

---

# Season Hook

Something moves offshore.

A purple tentacle rises from the water.

It waves.

Bentley squints.

"Do octopuses usually say hello?"

CUT TO BLACK.
