# BENTLEY'S DISCOVERY CLUB — CHANNEL ENGINE BIBLE
### The franchise operating system: built to get clicks, hold retention, earn repeat views, convert subs, stay AI-producible, and grow into a profitable kids' brand.

> Companion to `Bentley_Pilot_Episode_0_GREENLIGHT.md`. This document is deliverables **2–10**. The pilot script is deliverable **1**.

## 2. THE BENTLEY CHARACTER ENGINE
*A character isn't a personality list. It's a machine that generates story, comedy, emotion, and merch — forever.*

**The one-line engine: Bentley helps by *doing* — loudly, instantly, wrongly — until she learns to help by *listening.***

This single flaw→virtue does **five jobs at once**, which is why it scales:

| Job | How the engine delivers it |
|-----|----------------------------|
| **Story** | Every episode: rush in → make it worse → stop → listen → understand the real need → solve. Infinite animals, same reliable engine. |
| **Comedy** | Her confident *wrong* guesses ("Obviously you're a flower!") + Boxer physicality + snack obsession. |
| **Emotion** | Listening = empathy. The animal's *real* fear always mirrors a kid feeling (outgrowing things, being unheard, being new/scared). |
| **Education** | "Stop. Look. Listen." = literally the **scientific method** (observe before concluding). We teach *how to think*, not just facts. |
| **Brand/merch** | The catchphrase, the rally cry, and the **glowing Listening Shell** are ownable, repeatable, and sellable. |

### Bentley — the bible
- **Design:** Young female Boxer. Fawn coat, white chest blaze, one floppy ear (asymmetry = instant recognizability + easier AI consistency). Expressive brow. Collar with the **Listening Shell** charm.
- **Core want vs. need (every ep):** WANTS to fix it *now*. NEEDS to slow down and *hear* what's actually wrong.
- **Catchphrase (the method):** **"Stop. Look. Listen. ...Discover."**
- **Rally cry (the trigger):** **"Discovery Club — let's GO!"** (said before every adventure — kids will shout it).
- **Signature comedy:** (1) the **emergency snack** in her collar pouch — currency, distraction, plot device; (2) **confidently-wrong hypotheses** that let kids feel smart before correcting *with* her; (3) **Boxer physical comedy** — head-tilt, wiggle-butt, zoomies-when-overexcited (which often *cause* the complication).
- **The magic system (renewable + legible):** The **Listening Shell glows only when Bentley gets quiet and truly listens.** It then grants whatever the discovery needs — understanding animals (pilot), shrinking (bug episodes), breathing underwater (ocean), keeping up with a migration (butterflies/whales). *The power is the lesson.* One rule, infinite habitats.

### Stella the Sea Star — the recurring mentor-friend
Wise, funny, patient; an arm always *regrowing* (her visual reminder that "good things take time"). She inducts Bentley into the Club and gives her the shell. **Continuity trick:** in later non-tide-pool episodes, Stella can appear as a **shimmer-projection from the shell** (openly magical — keeps biology honest, keeps the duo alive everywhere, doubles the plush line).

### The "animal of the week" guest (e.g., Pip the hermit crab)
Each episode's guest is a **small character with a big feeling**. Pip = grumpy, tough, secretly scared he's "too small / outgrowing everything." The guest's emotional problem is the episode's heart; the guest's biology is the episode's lesson. This is the renewable slot.

## 3. THE REPEATABLE EPISODE FORMULA
*Same skeleton every time. Different animal every time. This is what makes a channel, not a one-off.*

**"THE BENTLEY METHOD" — 8-minute beat template:**

| Time | Beat | Always contains |
|------|------|-----------------|
| 0:00–0:12 | **Cold Hook** | A voice / glow / "Help!" / wonder. **Never** open on scenery. End on a question. |
| 0:12–0:35 | **Title sting + rally cry** | 2-note motif, logo, "Discovery Club — let's GO!" |
| 0:35–1:45 | **Flaw on display** | Bentley rushes in, guesses wrong, "helps" wrong → comedy. Meet the guest. |
| 1:45–3:15 | **Discovery 1 & 2 + it gets WORSE** | 2 dramatized facts that *raise* stakes. Her rushing backfires. |
| 3:15–4:30 | **THE TURN** | "Stop. Look. Listen." Shell glows. She hears the guest's *real* need. |
| 4:30–6:00 | **Climax: clock + setback** | A gentle ticking clock, ONE failure, then the listened-for solution + teamwork. Fact 3 is the mechanic. |
| 6:00–7:00 | **Emotional button + lesson** | The guest is moved; Bentley has changed; narrator lands the takeaway. |
| 7:00–7:45 | **Cliffhanger + CTA** | Shell glows toward next animal. "Where to next?!" Subscribe pull. |

**Every episode guarantees:** 1 cold hook · 3 dramatized facts · 1 flaw→listen arc · 1 clock-climax with a setback · 1 emotional button (animal's feeling mirrors a kid feeling) · 1 cliffhanger to the next ep · **≥5 pre-designed Shorts**.

**Season-long thread (light serialization = bingeability):** each cliffhanger hands off to the next animal (tentacle → octopus → ...). Plant a **season mystery** early (e.g., *why* are animals suddenly asking Bentley for help? Who's the Club really for?) and pay it off in the Ep 10 "Great Ocean Rescue" finale, where guests from every episode return. Recurring characters + a payoff = a reason to watch *in order* = watch-time + loyalty.

## 4. YOUTUBE TITLE & THUMBNAIL STRATEGY
*The video doesn't exist if nobody clicks. This gates everything upstream.*

### Title formula
`[BENTLEY + ACTION VERB IN CAPS] + [CURIOSITY GAP] + [emoji] | Bentley's Discovery Club`

- ✅ "Bentley Hears a VOICE in a Shell! 🐚 | Bentley's Discovery Club Ep 1"
- ✅ "What's HIDING in the Tide Pool?! 🌊🦀"
- ✅ "Bentley Can TALK to Sea Animals?! 🐶"
- ❌ "Bentley's Big Discovery" (no gap, no keyword — the original title; kill it).

Rules: curiosity gap (a question or a "?!"), one CAPS power-word, an emoji or two, a searchable noun ("tide pool," "octopus," "ants"), series tag for the algorithm. A/B two titles per upload; keep the winner.

### Thumbnail formula (the channel's visual signature)
- **Bentley's expressive face fills 40%+** of the frame — wide-eyed surprise/curiosity (the #1 CTR driver for kids).
- **Exactly ONE intriguing object** (glowing shell, peeking tentacle, the bug-eye macro).
- **High-saturation single-color background** (OC-sky blue for ocean eps, warm gold for desert, green for forest — color-codes the "world" at a glance).
- Big, simple, readable at thumbnail size. Optional small yellow "?" or "!" sticker.
- **Consistency = brand.** Same face-treatment, same logo bug, same font every time, so the channel is recognizable in a crowded sidebar.
- **Never** clickbait a payoff you don't deliver (parent trust + YouTube Kids policy).

### Series packaging
Same intro sting, same end card, same thumbnail grammar, episode numbers visible. A binge-watcher should *recognize a Bentley video in half a second.*

## 5. FIRST 30-SECOND RETENTION STRATEGY
*60–70% of viewers decide here. Win the first 30s or nothing else matters.*

1. **0–3s: the hook is a *sound or a face*, not a wide shot.** A whisper ("Help!"), a glow, Bentley's giant confused nose. Movement + mystery instantly.
2. **No establishing-shot preamble.** The beautiful sunrise is held *2 seconds, after the hook* — never before. (This was the original pilot's biggest retention leak: ~2 min of scenery before the magic.)
3. **A question by 0:10.** "Did the beach just TALK to me?!" Open a loop the brain needs closed.
4. **Branded sting fast (≤6s)** so it's a reward, not a toll booth. Pavlovian 2-note motif.
5. **Rally cry + first gag before 0:45.** Establish "this is fun AND funny" immediately.
6. **Tease the payoff:** within the first 30s the viewer should sense the episode's promise ("a tiny someone is in trouble and only Bentley can hear it").

## 6. MID-EPISODE RETENTION BEATS
*The 3:00–5:00 stretch is where kids' content dies. Engineer against it.*

- **Open-loop chaining:** never resolve a question without opening the next one. ("Not the ocean? Then what does he need?" → "Got the chain going — wait, the last shell's too SMALL!")
- **A beat every 30–45s:** gag, glow, reveal, or escalation. No flat stretches.
- **Escalation, not repetition:** each fact makes the problem *worse* before the turn (rushing backfires). Stakes climb.
- **The required setback:** the first solution must FAIL once (too-small shell). The dip-then-win is the single biggest retention spike in the episode.
- **Direct address:** "Discovery Club — what do *you* think Pip needs?" Pause a beat. Parasocial participation = comments + replays + watch-time.
- **The shell-glow as a dopamine cue:** a recurring audio-visual "ding" the audience learns to anticipate (progress signal).
- **Snack-gag punctuation:** drop a 3-sec comedy bead to reset attention between heavy beats.
- **Kill the lecture:** if a fact can't be *dramatized as plot or jeopardy*, cut it or move it to a Short. Facts must *do work* in the story.

## 7. SHORTS EXTRACTION PLAN
*Shorts are the discovery engine; long-form is the monetization engine. Design Shorts INTO the script, don't carve them out after.*

### Per-episode harvest (the pilot's 5, tagged ⚡ in the script)
1. **The hook:** "Did the beach just TALK to me?!" (mystery)
2. **The fail:** "Bentley's worst rescue ever" (comedy)
3. **The gag:** "I fed my snack to a FLOWER (it was not a flower)" (comedy + fact)
4. **The heart:** "I'm scared because I'm growing." / "You're not too small — you're just between shells." (emotion → the highest-share clip)
5. **The wow:** "Watch REAL hermit crabs trade houses" (the vacancy chain — fact/spectacle)

→ **5 Shorts per episode × 10 episodes = 50 Shorts** feeding the channel, each ending with "Full adventure on the channel →".

### Recurring Shorts *formats* (build sub-series, not one-offs)
- **"Did You Know with Bentley"** — 20-sec single-fact bombs (sea stars regrow arms!). Evergreen, searchable, algorithm-friendly.
- **"Bentley's Wrong Guesses"** — comedy reel of confident-wrong moments. Personality-builder.
- **"Stop. Look. Listen."** — 15-sec mindful/SEL micro-moments (parents share these).
- **"Tiny Big Feelings"** — the emotional buttons (outgrowing things, being new). The shareable, parent-trust clips.

### Shorts → long-form funnel
Every Short: cold-opens on the spike (no intro), captioned for sound-off, ends on a curiosity gap + "Full story on the channel." Pin the long-form in comments. Shorts farm reach; the pinned link and end-screen convert to watch-time and subs.

## 8. PARENT-TRUST & EDUCATIONAL VALUE STRATEGY
*Parents are the gatekeepers and the wallet. Trust = subscribes, repeat views, and "it's okay to leave this on."*

- **Accuracy is non-negotiable.** Every fact real and source-checkable (sea-star regeneration, anemones-are-animals, the hermit-crab vacancy chain — all genuine). One viral "that's wrong" comment costs trust. Keep a per-episode **fact source list**.
- **Teach thinking, not just trivia.** "Stop. Look. Listen." models *observation before conclusion* — the scientific method in toddler-grammar. The brand's deeper promise: *we teach kids how to be curious.*
- **SEL built in (the Bluey lane):** every episode's emotional core is a real kid feeling — outgrowing things, feeling unheard, being new and scared, patience ("good things take time"). Empathy = listening, modeled by the hero.
- **Calm, kind, safe:** gentle stakes (a clock, never violence or fear-bait), warm resolutions, no jump-scares, soft sound design. Conflict is *misunderstanding*, resolved by understanding.
- **Parent/teacher surface:** description includes a **"Discovery Notes"** block — the 3 facts, the feeling/SEL theme, and a "Try this at home" prompt ("next time you visit a tide pool, *stop and watch* one animal for a whole minute — what is it doing?"). Makes the channel classroom- and bedtime-safe.
- **End recap card:** a 5-sec "Today we discovered..." reinforces learning (and signals value to parents skimming).
- **Compliance:** clean COPPA/"Made for Kids" setup, no manipulative dark-pattern CTAs, age-appropriate everything.

## 9. AI PRODUCTION FEASIBILITY NOTES
*Pipeline: Claude (script/shotlist) → Higgsfield (image→video) → AI voice → CapCut/Premiere (assembly). Designed around what AI does well and *away* from what it doesn't.*

### The #1 risk: character consistency. Solve it first.
- Build a locked **Bentley reference sheet** (turnarounds, expressions, the one floppy ear + white blaze as anchor features) and reuse the same reference/seed every shot.
- Same for Stella, Pip, and each habitat. **Maintain a "style lock" doc** so every episode looks like the same show.

### Design choices that make AI production cheap and consistent
- **Narrator-VO-forward; minimize lip-sync.** Talking animals use *subtle* mouth motion + VO, not full articulated lip-sync (AI's weakest area, the uncanny zone). Bentley "speaks" often in reaction + narration.
- **Short shots (2–4s).** Easier to generate cleanly, easier to fix, better pacing anyway.
- **Fixed background plates per habitat.** Generate the tide pool once; reuse. Cuts cost and locks continuity.
- **The shell-glow = one reusable VFX** (cheap, repeatable, on-brand).
- **Limit characters-per-frame.** Multi-character acting in one shot is the hard/expensive case — stage conversations as intercut singles.

### Easy / Hard / Expensive
- **Easy:** establishing/nature plates, macro beauty shots, atmosphere, AI narrator VO, the glow VFX, single-character reaction shots.
- **Hard:** Bentley consistency across ~120 shots, talking-animal mouths, multi-character framing, continuity of one environment.
- **Expensive:** long continuous action, heavy regeneration to fight character drift, water/underwater sim.
- **Mitigations:** reference-lock everything; VO over lip-sync; short shots; reuse plates; intercut over group shots; batch-generate then assemble.

### Repeatable production line (so 10+ episodes are realistic)
Claude expands beat-sheet → shotlist + VO script → batch image gen (locked refs) → image→video → AI voice → CapCut assembly → captions + the 5 Shorts. Templatize once; every episode flows the same pipe. **Score: this format is deliberately built to be ~7/10 feasible instead of the original's 5/10.**

## 10. FRANCHISE & MONETIZATION RECOMMENDATIONS
*Where the money is — and the order to unlock it.*

### The revenue ladder (sequence matters)
1. **Shorts** → reach & discovery (free funnel).
2. **Long-form watch-time** → ad revenue (the daily engine).
3. **Subscribers + channel memberships** → recurring base, members-only Shorts/coloring pages.
4. **Music** → the theme + a per-episode **"fact song"** earworm. Drives retention AND earns on YouTube Music/Spotify AND is the cheapest marketing you have. ("Stop, Look, Listen" + "The Vacancy Chain (Hermit Crab Song)").
5. **Merch** → hero product = the **glowing Listening Shell necklace**; then **Bentley plush**, **Stella plush**, **collectible animal-of-the-week figures** (built-in "gotta collect 'em all" — one per episode).
6. **Books** → nonfiction tie-ins ("Bentley's Discovery Club: Tide Pools"), board books, "Stop Look Listen" activity/sticker books.
7. **Educational products** → preschool curriculum kits, teacher guides, an app of mini "listen and discover" games.
8. **Licensing & sponsorship** → aquariums/zoos/museums, eco & nature orgs (Nat Geo Kids / WWF co-brand), and **pet brands** (a Boxer hero is a natural fit). Live "Discovery Club" experiences at aquariums.

### Why this IP is built to monetize
- **Collectibility is structural:** one new animal per episode = one new figure/plush/book per episode. The format *is* a product line.
- **The hero object** (Listening Shell) is both the magic system and the #1 toy — kids will want the glowing shell that "lets you hear animals."
- **The method is licensable** ("Stop. Look. Listen." curriculum) — sells to parents *and* schools.
- **Evergreen + safe + accurate** = long-tail ad revenue and institutional partners who won't touch edgier kids' content.

### KPIs to watch (the dashboard)
CTR (target 4–8%+), **avg % viewed (the real signal — protect the 3–5 min zone)**, 30-sec retention, Shorts→subscribe conversion, returning-viewer %, and *binge rate* (do they watch Ep 2 after Ep 1?). Let the retention graphs — not taste — pick titles, thumbnails, cold opens, and the 8-min vs 5-min cut.

### Launch sequencing (recommendation)
Don't drip one episode. **Bank 5–6 episodes + 25–30 Shorts before launch**, release the pilot + Ep 1 together (so the cliffhanger pays off instantly = binge from day one), then a steady weekly cadence with daily Shorts feeding the funnel. A channel needs a *shelf*, not a single video.
