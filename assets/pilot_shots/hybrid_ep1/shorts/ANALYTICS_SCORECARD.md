# Analytics Scorecard — Episode 1 Shorts Launch

Fill this in from **YouTube Studio → Analytics** at each checkpoint. The goal isn't vanity views — it's finding **which hook archetype** to scale for Episode 2. On a zero-sub channel, the make-or-break number is **swipe-away rate in the first 3 seconds.**

## What each metric means here
- **Viewed vs. swiped (3s hold %)** = % of viewers still watching at 3s. *The cold-start KPI.* Studio: "viewed vs. swiped away."
- **Avg view duration** (seconds) and **Avg % viewed** = retention depth.
- **Replays** = loops; weighted heavily by the Shorts algorithm (comedy/cliffhanger should win here).
- **Subs gained** = attributed to that Short.
- **Ep1 traffic from Shorts** = Ep1 views/day bump on/after that Short's day (your only conversion proxy — clickthrough is blocked on MFK). Track Ep1 "Traffic source: Shorts" if available.
- **Returning viewers %** = channel stickiness.

## Targets (new MFK channel, rough guide)
| Metric | Soft pass | Good | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3s hold | >60% | >70% | >80% |
| Avg % viewed | >50% | >65% | **>80–90%** |
| Replays / view | >0.15 | >0.3 | >0.5 |
| Ep1 daily-views bump after a Short | any lift | +25% | +100% |

> **Aim for 90%+ average % viewed.** These Shorts are only 12–17s — a tight Short that hooks should be watched nearly to the end. Record each Short's numbers at **7 days**, then declare the **winning category** (science / comedy / mystery / character / emotion / cliffhanger) and weight Episode 2's 4 Shorts toward it.
