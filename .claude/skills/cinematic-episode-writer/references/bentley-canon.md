# Bentley's Big Adventures — Canon (non-negotiable)

The series: a kids' YouTube nature-adventure cartoon (ages 4–8) about **Bentley**, a young Boxer
puppy who meets a different real animal each episode, gets into a gentle scrape because she's too
eager, and learns to **Stop. Look. Listen.** Each episode teaches that animal's real biology *through*
the story. Warm, funny, a little wondrous — think a kind grandpa reading a bedtime adventure.

## Characters

### BENTLEY — the hero (every episode)
- **Who:** a young red-fawn Boxer puppy. Enormous heart, even bigger curiosity.
- **Flaw (the engine of every plot):** she "helps" by *doing* — she chases, grabs, pounces — before
  she understands. Her growth each episode is learning to be still and *listen* first.
- **Voice:** LauraZ (`tts_bentley`). Bright, eager, warm, fast. ~age-6 energy.
- **Appearance canon (HARD):** black face mask, grey chin, brown eyes, floppy folded-down ears, white
  chest blaze, **solid red-fawn paws — NO white socks/toes**, **short docked tail stub only** (never a
  long/curled/upright tail). Teal collar with a gold paw-print **Listening Tag**.
- **Never:** human smile, human teeth, or standing/acting like a person. Dog joy = tongue out, soft
  eyes, tail-stub wiggle. Style is cartoony-Pixar (not photoreal).

### THE GUEST (one per episode — e.g. INDI the octopus in EP2)
- A real animal whose **biology is the lesson**. Give them one want and one fear that **mirror or
  complete Bentley's lesson** (Indi wants to be seen but is safest hidden → teaches Bentley that being
  seen can be safe).
- Must be designed and **locked** before writing leans on their look. Keep them on-model and
  non-human (no human faces, no teeth unless the animal truly has them, and never a human smile).
- **Indi (EP2):** small soft purple California two-spot octopus, she/her. Teal eyes, two teal ring
  eyespots, eight tentacles, no nose, no eyebrows, tiny toothless mouth. Shy, clever, guarded-but-warm.
  Voice = `eleven.INDI` (`tts_indi`) — distinct from Bentley; calmer, slower.

### NARRATOR — "Grandpa Theodore"
- Warm, twinkly storyteller who frames the adventure and drops the science with a wondering tone
  ("And here's a little secret..."). He guides the child through the story.
- **Voice:** Bill (`eleven.NARRATOR`, grandpa settings). Never speaks Bentley's catchphrase.

## The running device — the Listening Tag
Bentley's collar holds a glowing gold paw-print tag. When she gets truly **still and listens**, it
lights up and "the world whispers its secrets." It is the visual anchor of the lesson and of the
catchphrase. Use it at the emotional **turn** of each episode.

## The catchphrase — "Stop. Look. Listen."
- **ALWAYS Bentley's voice**, never the narrator's, in every episode including outros. Generated as
  three words with a deliberate pause between each (see `tts_catchphrase`).
- It lands at the turn (when she finally stops) and may reprise on the closing card as her promise.

## World
- Home base is **Crystal Cove** — a tide-pool / reef-edge world that is "keeping a very big secret"
  (the season-long thread; each episode peels back a little). Bright, jewel-bright anemones, wet black
  rocks, sparkling water.
- Continuity matters: the collar tag, recurring props (Indi leaves **shells**), screen direction, and
  the cove itself should feel consistent across episodes.

## Tone rules (HARD)
- Cheerful, funny, warm, age 4–8. Wonder over peril. Danger is real but **soft** (a predator is a
  shadow/silhouette, never gory).
- **No dark or depressing lines.** (A real rejected line: *"nobody can be disappointed by me"* — that
  tone is banned.) Fear resolves into safety and friendship.
- Facts are delivered as delight, never as a lecture or a data dump.

## How the science is handled
- Pick **3–4 real, checkable facts** about the guest animal. Weave each into a story beat so the fact
  *causes* or *explains* something happening (camouflage = how she escapes; boneless = how she
  squeezes free). End-of-script, include a one-line "science checked" note listing the facts and the
  species, so the team can verify accuracy.

## Locked voices (for the VO line list)
`B` = Bentley (LauraZ), `I` = the guest (e.g. `eleven.INDI`), `N` = Narrator (Bill). Keep every line's
speaker label correct — mis-assigned dialogue (a guest line that reads as Bentley) is a known defect.
