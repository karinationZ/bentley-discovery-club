# Bentley's Discovery Club — 90-Day Production & Credit Budget Plan

> Goal: launch a Made-for-Kids educational animation channel sustainably on **Higgsfield Ultra**, banking a buffer before going public, then settling into a weekly rhythm that the credits can actually support.

## Assumptions & cost model
- **Plan:** Higgsfield Ultra — **3,000 credits/mo** base; slider to 6,000 / 9,000; one-time top-up packs (500 / 1k / 2k / 4k) available.
- **Cost rule of thumb:** **~3.5 credits per second** of finished Seedance 2.0 Fast 720p video; add **~40% for revisions/retakes**. Keyframe **stills ≈ free** (unlimited image models). **Shorts cut from episode footage ≈ free** (edit only).
- **Format discipline (the key to affordability):** the **pilot is a 7–8 min flagship** (expensive, one-time). **Regular episodes are lean 3–5 min.** Shorts are **reused** from episode footage. This is what keeps weekly cadence inside Ultra.
- **Channel is Made for Kids (COPPA)** — growth leans on Shorts + thumbnails + playlists; money leans on volume + merch + songs, not ad RPM.

### Per-asset credit reference
| Asset | Final length | ~Credits (incl. ~40% revisions) |
|---|---|---|
| Cold-open teaser | 60–90s | ~350–500 |
| **Pilot (flagship)** | 7–8 min | ~2,000–2,400 |
| Regular episode (lean) | 3–5 min | ~800–1,400 |
| Short — reused from episode | 20–35s | **~0 (edit only)** |
| Short — standalone new footage | 20–35s | ~100–150 |

## MONTH 1 — Foundation & Flagship (Weeks 1–4)
**Goal:** channel built + branded; pilot produced; first Shorts banked. **Publish nothing yet** — bank the buffer.
- **Week 1:** Channel setup via the Launch Kit (name, branding, Made-for-Kids, playlists, SEO). Produce the **cold-open teaser** (~400 cr). Lock metadata templates.
- **Weeks 2–3:** Produce the full **PILOT** (~2,200 cr): keyframes (free) → Seedance Fast clips → assemble + AI voice + music.
- **Week 4:** Harvest **6 Shorts** from pilot footage (~free). Build playlists + thumbnails.
- **Month 1 credits: ~2,600** → fits Ultra base (3,000). ✅
- **Published:** nothing (optionally set the teaser as the channel trailer).

## MONTH 2 — Bank & Soft Launch (Weeks 5–8)
**Goal:** two lean episodes banked; launch with a cluster so day-one viewers can binge.
- **Weeks 5–6:** **Episode 1** (lean 3–5 min, ~1,100 cr).
- **Week 7:** **Episode 2** (~1,100 cr).
- **Week 8:** 🚀 **SOFT LAUNCH** — publish **Pilot + Episode 1 together** (cliffhanger → binge). Begin **daily Shorts** drip (reused). Harvest ~10 Shorts.
- **Month 2 credits: ~2,300** → fits Ultra base. ✅
- **Published:** Pilot + Ep 1 + daily Shorts.

## MONTH 3 — Cadence & Optimize (Weeks 9–12)
**Goal:** establish the weekly rhythm; let data drive decisions.
- **Week 9:** Publish **Ep 2**. Produce **Ep 3** (~1,100 cr).
- **Week 10:** Publish **Ep 3**. Produce **Ep 4** (~1,100 cr).
- **Week 11:** Publish **Ep 4**. Produce **Ep 5** (~1,100 cr).
- **Week 12:** Review analytics; re-cut thumbnails/titles on underperformers; produce **Ep 6** (~1,100 cr). Add ~4 standalone "Did You Know" fact Shorts (~500 cr).
- Daily Shorts throughout (reused).
- **Month 3 credits: ~3,400–3,900** → **slightly over base.** Action: bump the Ultra slider to ~4,000, **or** add one **1,000-credit top-up.** ⚠️

---

## 90-day credit total: ~8,300–8,800 credits
Ultra base supplies **9,000 over 3 months**, so the plan **just fits** — with Month 3 needing a small slider bump or one top-up once you're in weekly cadence. Everything before launch stays inside base Ultra.

## When to scale credits (triggers)
- **Weekly 7–8 min episodes** (not the lean format) → you need the **6k–9k slider + top-ups** (~7,000–9,500 cr/mo). Reserve full length for occasional *specials*.
- **High revision weeks** (new habitats, complex multi-character shots) → one top-up pack.
- **Traction arrives** (rising views/revenue) → scale credits to match. *Don't buy capacity ahead of demand.*

## KPIs to watch monthly
CTR (target 4–8%), **average % viewed** (protect the 3–5 min zone), 30-sec retention, **Shorts→subscribe** conversion, returning-viewer %, and **binge rate** (do they watch Ep 2 after Ep 1?). Let the graphs — not taste — pick titles, thumbnails, and episode length.

## The one rule
Match spend to traction, not ambition. Pre-audience you need a killer pilot + a steady Shorts drip + a few episodes — all of which base Ultra handles. Scale up only when the numbers justify it.
