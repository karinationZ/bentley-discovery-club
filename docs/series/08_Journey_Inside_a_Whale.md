# Journey Inside a Whale
### Season 1 · Episode 8 · Bentley's Discovery Club

> **Logline:** Chasing a song she thinks is a cry for help, Bentley gets accidentally scooped up in a whale's giant gulp — and discovers she's perfectly safe, the song was never sad, and the biggest animal on Earth feels achingly alone.

**Habitat:** The open ocean, with a migrating gray/blue whale · **Power used:** Listening + underwater + shrinking · **Guest:** **Mara**, an enormous, gentle whale separated from her pod.
**The kid-feeling we're really telling (SEL):** *Feeling isolated and "too big/too much" to be understood — and learning your voice reaches farther than you think.*
**Bentley's arc:** Hears the song, assumes "she's drowning, SAVE HER!" and charges in loud (doing) → gets gulped, panics → learns she's safe, and that her own noise was *drowning out* Mara's call → goes quiet so the real song can carry.

## The 3 Dramatized Facts (real)
1. The blue whale is the **largest animal that has ever lived** — its **heart is the size of a small car**, and its **tongue can weigh as much as an elephant.**
2. Baleen whales eat tiny **krill** by gulping huge mouthfuls of water and filtering it through **baleen** — and their **throat is far too small to swallow a person.** (You'd be perfectly safe — and gently spat back out.)
3. **Whale song travels for hundreds of miles** underwater — whales call to friends and family across entire oceans. Whales must **surface to breathe** through a blowhole.

## Cold Hook (0:00–0:12)
Tiny Bentley in endless blue. A wall of darkness rises in front of her — then slowly, an **eye the size of a window** opens and looks right at her. **BENTLEY:** *(whisper)* "...That's not an island. That's an *EYEBALL.*"

## The Bentley Method
- **Flaw on display:** Sure the song means "drowning," Bentley swims straight into the whale's path shouting "I'LL SAVE YOU!" — and gets swept up in a gulp of krill. Inside, she braces to be swallowed... and isn't. (Comedy: she delivers a heroic farewell speech to no one before realizing she's totally fine and slightly ticklish.)
- **Discovery + it gets worse:** Safely spat into a cove, Bentley keeps "helping" loudly — barking, splashing, organizing a "rescue" — and her racket smothers Mara's song so it can't carry. Mara's far-off pod stops answering.
- **THE TURN:** Stop. Look. Listen. Shell glows. Bentley goes silent and *hears* the song for what it is: *"It's not a sad song. It's me saying 'I'm here' — over and over — to a family that's too far to hear me anymore."*
- **Climax — clock + setback:** Night falls; if Mara doesn't reconnect before the currents split them for good, she'll migrate alone. Bentley's loud "HEY, OVER HERE!" plan fails (still too much noise). Setback → Bentley quiets *everything*, even herself, and lets Mara's pure low song roll out across the dark water. Far away — a single answering call. Then many.
- **Emotional button:** "Your song was never sad, Mara. It was you, reaching across the *whole ocean,* saying 'I'm here.' ...And somebody finally heard you."

## Signature Scene (dialogue)
> **MARA:** "I'm the biggest thing for a thousand miles. And somehow that just makes me the *loneliest.*"
> **BENTLEY:** "I used to think helping meant being the loudest. But you taught me something, Mara. Sometimes the bravest thing you can do is get *quiet*... and trust that your voice will find the right ears."
> *(a distant whale-call answers across the deep)*
> **BENTLEY:** "...See? Told you somebody was listening."

## Cliffhanger → Episode 9
Mara's reunited song ripples for miles — and lands on a meadow near the coast, where the flowers are strangely shut tight and a single exhausted bee bumps into Bentley. *"No nectar... hive's starving... can't find the far field..."* The shell glows toward the meadow. → *The Secret Life of Bees.*

## Parent-trust note
Make "swallowed" instantly safe and even funny — the real fact (throat too small) defuses any fear within seconds. No darkness-as-danger; frame the inside as wondrous, then quickly back to open water.

## AI production note
**Easy:** scale-awe (tiny Bentley vs. vast whale) and the eye reveal — top-tier AI shots. Whale song = audio-driven, cheap and moving. **Hard:** a full animated whale body in motion + interior shots. **Mitigation:** favor partial reveals (eye, fluke, silhouette), use light shafts and particulate water to imply scale, keep the "inside" brief and stylized.
