# Bentley — PERFORMANCE BIBLE (the star's playbook)
> Locked 2026-06-16. This is HOW Bentley performs in every episode — her moves, her voice, her lines, and the rules that keep her the star. Pairs with `assets/character_design/LOCKED_ELEMENTS.md` (how she *looks*). Look = Locked Elements. **Performance = this doc.**

---

## 0. WHO SHE IS (one breath)
**Bentley is a young, red-fawn Boxer with an enormous heart who helps first and learns to listen.** Curious, impulsive, brave, a little goofy — she throws herself at every problem, usually gets it adorably *wrong* first, then slows down and discovers the real wonder. Kids see themselves in her: big feelings, good intentions, learning as she goes. **She is the lens. Nature happens TO her, and we feel it through her face.**

Her flaw → her growth, every episode: **acts before she understands → learns to Stop, Look, Listen.** (Keep the growth *small* each time — she never becomes a perfect little scientist; she's always learning.)

## 2. HER VOICE
- **TTS:** Inworld **Abby** (warm, bright, young-but-not-squeaky), **+12% tempo** so she feels quick and eager, not sleepy.
- **Speech tics (write these into her lines):**
  - Talks to the creatures like instant friends — *"Hi! I'm Bentley. What's wrong, little guy?"*
  - Thinks out loud / talks to herself — *"Okay okay okay… what would help?"*
  - Breaks the 4th wall to the kids — *"Did you SEE that?!"*
  - Reframes scary/hard as wonder — *"Complicated's just a wonder we haven't figured out yet."*
  - Gentle, never mean; her jokes are at her own expense, never the creature's.

## 4. THE "BENTLEY IS THE STAR" SHOOTING RULES (non-negotiable)
This is what fixes "a nature doc a dog wanders into."
1. **Open AND close every scene on Bentley's face.** First face in, last face out.
2. **Reaction-shot rule:** after EVERY reveal/fact, cut to Bentley reacting. The fact isn't the shot — *her face discovering the fact* is the shot. (This single rule ~doubles her screen time.)
3. **~70% of shots contain Bentley.** Creatures/science are what she's reacting TO.
4. **No fact narrated over emptiness.** The narrator never explains over a bare tide pool — he explains while we watch *Bentley* discover it.
5. **Her POV is the camera's bias** — when in doubt, frame the world the way *she'd* see it (low, close, curious).
6. **She drives the plot:** she has a goal each episode (help the creature); the science is the *obstacle in her quest*, never a lecture.

---

## 5. HER EPISODE ARC (the repeatable engine)
Every episode runs this shape so she grows a little each time:
1. **Wonder** — Bentley notices something nobody else did (cold open on her).
2. **Helps-wrong** — she rushes in with the obvious fix… which is adorably wrong (this is where the *real science* corrects her).
3. **The turn** — she stops. *"Stop. Look. Listen."* (collar glows). She finally pays attention.
4. **The wonder revealed** — the real fact unfolds; the WHOAA jaw-drop.
5. **Home / heart** — she helps for real; the Soft-Eyes Lean; a friend is safe.
6. **The tag** — to camera: *"Stop. Look. Listen… and you'll find the wonder, everywhere."* → cliffhanger.

## 6. HER RELATIONSHIPS (the ensemble orbits her)
- **Pip** (hermit crab, Ep1 guest): the friend she helps-wrong then helps-right. Scared-not-angry; she's his big brave goofy protector.
- **Stella** (sea star, mentor): the calm voice who nudges Bentley toward *Stop-Look-Listen* — Stella never solves it, she helps Bentley solve it.
- **The purple octopus** (season cliffhanger): the recurring "something's watching" hook past the rocks.
- **The kids (camera):** her best friends. She lets them in on everything.

## 7. CONSISTENCY ANCHORS (so she's the same dog forever)
- **Element:** always embed Bentley-MASTER `<<<1c82ee5b-8713-4596-80ec-6ce854502a4d>>>` + restate features + *"same dog, match the model sheet exactly."*
- **Paws:** `solid red-fawn legs and all four paws — NO white socks, NO white toes` in EVERY prompt. White is ONLY the chest blaze + the glowing collar shell.
- **Proportions:** never prompt "big eyes" → say *"soulful, well-proportioned eyes, balanced head, correct short muzzle, athletic build — NOT googly, NOT oversized."*
- **Pull 2–3 takes** on every hero shot; reject any off-model proportions or stray socks.
- **One grade across the episode** so every shot feels like the same film.

## 8. HOW A BENTLEY-ISM BECOMES A SHOT (pipeline)
1. **Keyframe** (Nano-Banana, cheap) on the locked element + the move's prompt recipe → pick the best of 2–3.
2. **Animate:** hero/emotional beats → **Cinema Studio 3.0** (cinematic, smooth); simple inserts → **Hailuo** (cheap).
3. **Talking close-ups → Lip-Sync Studio** (her VO drives the mouth). *(MCP audio path is currently erroring; web Lip-Sync Studio is the confirmed route.)*
4. **Finish:** **upscale to 1080p/60fps** (smooths motion — the single biggest "looks pro" step; ~free).
5. **Reuse:** save the best take as a **Motion-Control reference** so the move is identical next episode.
