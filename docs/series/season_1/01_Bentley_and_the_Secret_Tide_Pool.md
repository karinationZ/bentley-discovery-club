# Bentley and the Secret Tide Pool
### Season 1 · Episode 1 · Bentley's Big Adventures

> **Logline:** Bentley returns to the tide pool and discovers a dark hidden crevice — and a glowing neon sea slug everyone avoids, who's lonely because the whole pool thinks her dazzling colors make her "too weird to be near."

**Habitat:** The deep crevice of the Crystal Cove tide pool · **Power used:** Listening (talk to animals) · **Guest:** **Nudi**, a Spanish shawl nudibranch (electric purple + orange).
**The kid-feeling we're really telling (SEL):** *Feeling "too different / too much" and left out — and learning your difference is your strength.*
**Bentley's arc:** Judges Nudi by her looks ("bright = scary, stay back!") → learns to listen past appearances → discovers Nudi's colors *protect the whole pool.*

## The 3 Dramatized Facts (real, source-checkable)
1. **Nudibranchs wear warning colors on purpose** (aposematism): bright = "please don't nibble me, I taste bad." It's a polite warning, not an attack.
2. Some nudibranchs **eat stinging anemones and reuse the stingers** for their own defense — they borrow their armor.
3. They're called "naked gills" — they **breathe through the feathery plumes on their backs.**

## Cold Hook (0:00–0:12)
Dark crevice. A flash of impossible neon streaks past Bentley's nose. **BENTLEY:** "...Did a *rainbow* just swim past me?" The glow vanishes into the dark. A tiny voice: *"Go away. Everybody does."*

## The Bentley Method
- **Flaw on display:** Bentley sees the neon glow, assumes DANGER, herds the little snails "to safety," and accidentally corners the very creature she's scared of. Comedy: she "warns" everyone about a monster that turns out to be the size of a grape.
- **Discovery + it gets worse:** She learns Nudi is avoided by everyone. Bentley "fixes" it by trying to *scrub Nudi's colors off* to help her "fit in" → mortifying, makes Nudi hide deeper.
- **THE TURN:** Stop. Look. Listen. Shell glows. Bentley finally hears it: *"I'm not weird. I'm warning everyone I'm not a snack. But it also means... nobody ever gets close."*
- **Climax — clock + setback:** High tide rushes in and a hungry fish hunts a baby snail. Bentley's body-block fails (she's underwater, clumsy). Setback → she remembers Nudi's colors are a *warning*, positions glowing Nudi as the lookout-beacon; the fish reads the "danger" signal and veers off. Nudi *saves the pool.*
- **Emotional button:** "Your colors didn't keep everyone away. They kept everyone *safe.* You were protecting us this whole time."

## Signature Scene (dialogue)
> **NUDI:** "Everyone swims the other way. They think bright means bad."
> **BENTLEY:** "I thought that too. For like... a really embarrassing five minutes."
> **NUDI:** "So why'd you come back?"
> **BENTLEY:** *(sits, gets still)* "Because I stopped looking *at* you and started listening *to* you. And — whoa. You're not scary. You're a *lighthouse.*"

## Cliffhanger → Episode 2
As the tide settles, a **purple tentacle** slips a single spiral shell onto the rock — a *message* — then withdraws into the deep. **BENTLEY:** "Okay, that's the third time. Who keeps leaving me *mail?*" → *The Octopus Escape Artist.*
