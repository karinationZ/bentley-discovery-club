# The Shark Who Was Afraid
### Season 1 · Episode 6 · Bentley's Big Adventures

> **Logline:** Everyone flees the giant shadow in the shallows — but the "monster" is Finn, a young shark so gentle and lonely that he's more scared of *them* than they'll ever be of him. Then Finn gets tangled in a net, and the only one brave enough to listen is Bentley.

**Habitat:** The kelp shallows off the cove · **Power used:** Listening + underwater · **Guest:** **Finn**, a young leopard shark (gentle, shy).
**The kid-feeling we're really telling (SEL):** *Being misjudged and labeled "scary/bad" before anyone gets to know you — and the courage to look past a reputation.*
**Bentley's arc:** Believes the scary rumors and tries to "protect everyone *from*" Finn (acting on fear, not facts) → learns Finn is lonely and kind → listens past the label.

## The 3 Dramatized Facts (real)
1. **Most sharks are harmless to people and many are shy.** The biggest sharks of all — whale sharks and basking sharks — eat **tiny plankton.**
2. Sharks have a **sixth sense: electroreception** — special pores let them feel the faint electricity of a heartbeat in the water.
3. Sharks are **older than dinosaurs *and* older than trees** — over 400 million years — and they grow and lose **thousands of teeth** across their lives.

## Cold Hook (0:00–0:12)
A huge dark shape glides under Bentley. Fish scatter screaming "SHARK!" The shape stops. A small, wobbly voice comes from it: *"...please don't run away too."*

## The Bentley Method
- **Flaw on display:** Bentley rallies a panicked "defense squad," tries to herd everyone away and *warn off* the shark — treating Finn as the villain without ever speaking to him. (Comedy: she draws up a dramatic battle plan against a shark who is, at that moment, politely waiting to say hi.)
- **Discovery + it gets worse:** Her shooing spooks Finn into a current — and straight into an abandoned tangle of net. Now he's trapped, and everyone's still too scared to come close.
- **THE TURN:** Stop. Look. Listen. Shell glows. Bentley feels Finn's racing heartbeat through the water (electroreception, mirrored back at *her*) and hears him: *"Everyone always swims away before I can even say hello. I just wanted one friend."*
- **Climax — clock + setback:** The tide is dragging the netted shark toward the rocks. Bentley tries to bite the net free alone — too strong, fails, and she gets scared too. Setback → she calls the animals she's met (Indi the octopus arrives to pick the knots!) and they free Finn *together.* Then the "monster" uses his strength to lift a trapped friend to safety — hero turn.
- **Emotional button:** "You're not a monster, Finn. You were just lonely. Everybody got scared before they got to *know* you. I'm really glad I didn't."

## Signature Scene (dialogue)
> **FINN:** "It's okay. You can run. Everybody runs."
> **BENTLEY:** "...I'm not gonna lie, my legs *really* wanted to. But then I heard your heart going *boom-boom-boom* faster than mine. Monsters aren't scared, Finn. *You're* scared. Which means you're not a monster. You're just a kid. Like me."

## Cliffhanger → Episode 7
Freed and grateful, Finn warns them: out past the kelp, the water's getting strange — and it's not just the sea. A hot, dry wind blows in off the land, carrying a single impossible wildflower petal. The shell turns inland, toward the desert. → *The Desert That Blooms Overnight.*
