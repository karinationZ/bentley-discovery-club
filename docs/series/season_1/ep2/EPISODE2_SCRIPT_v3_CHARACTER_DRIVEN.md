# THE OCTOPUS ESCAPE ARTIST — v3 Character-Driven Restart

*Bentley's Big Adventures* — Season 1, Episode 2  
Target runtime: **3:30-4:00**  
Primary feeling: **fun, curious, playful, cute, adventurous, educational**

This script supersedes `EPISODE2_SCRIPT_v2.md` for the restart build. v2 was still a salvage/resequence script. v3 is a fresh character-driven script built around Bentley and Indi sharing scenes, talking, reacting, and discovering together.

Production recording note: use `EP2_v3_PRODUCTION_CUE_SHEET.md` for the locked VO cut and shot list. The dialogue below is the master story script; the cue sheet is the runtime-controlled production version.

## Production Promise

This episode is not a science montage. It is a two-friend tidepool adventure.

The camera should keep Bentley and Indi together as much as possible:

- Two-shot first.
- Reaction second.
- Insert only when motivated.
- Return to the relationship.

No static character scenes. No black transition dips. No sad default Bentley. No slow sad music bed.

## Character Tone

**Bentley:** curious, excited, playful, expressive, helpful, a little impulsive, never gloomy by default.

**Indi:** clever, funny, friendly, slightly mysterious, proud of being hard to find, cautious when the eel appears.

**Narrator:** warm adult storyteller. Brief, supportive, never carrying whole scenes alone.

## Science Through Story

| Fact | How v3 uses it |
| --- | --- |
| Camouflage: color and texture | Indi turns it into a hide-and-seek game with Bentley. |
| Eel predator | Indi explains why hiding matters, then the eel interrupts the game. |
| Taste by touch | Indi reads Bentley's paw/tag before trusting her. |
| Boneless body | Indi solves the rescue by squeezing through a tiny gap. |
| Three hearts / blue blood | A cozy friendship button after the adventure, not the main plot. |

## Scene Pod 1 — The Rock Winked

**Setting:** Bright tidepool morning. Wet rocks, jewel anemones, little ripples. Playful mystery music.

**Production intent:** Start fun and curious. Bentley should be energetic and adorable, not cautious-sad. Use a two-shot as soon as Indi reveals herself.

**EXT. CRYSTAL COVE TIDEPOOL — MORNING**

Bentley trots into frame, nose low, tail-stub wiggling. She spots a speckled "rock" with one suspicious purple edge.

**BENTLEY**  
Helloooo, little rock. You look like you need... a boop.

Bentley lifts one paw.

The rock opens one huge eye.

Bentley freezes with her paw in the air.

The eye blinks.

**BENTLEY**  
You winked.

The "rock" ripples purple. Indi peeks out, tiny smile, two teal ring spots glowing softly.

**INDI**  
Rocks are very good winkers.

Bentley's face lights up.

**BENTLEY**  
You are not a rock.

**INDI**  
I am when I want to be.

Indi poofs a tiny puff of ink, then pops out beside the rock, pleased with herself.

**BENTLEY**  
I'm Bentley.

**INDI**  
I'm Indi. And I am impossible to find.

**BENTLEY**  
I found you.

Indi considers this. She narrows one eye, playful.

**INDI**  
You found one eyeball. That barely counts.

Bentley gasps, delighted.

**BENTLEY**  
There are rules?

Indi slides onto the rock like a tiny game-show host.

**INDI**  
Of course there are rules. Rule one: no booping the champion.

**BENTLEY**  
Champion of what?

Indi grins with her eyes, not a big mouth.

**INDI**  
Hide-and-seek.

Music pops brighter.

## Scene Pod 2 — Hide-And-Seek Champion

**Setting:** Same tidepool pocket. Keep them together. This should feel like a game, not a lecture.

**Production intent:** Show color and texture transformation in a shared scene. Bentley watches and reacts while Indi performs. No isolated static macro shot.

Indi points one arm to the rock behind her.

**INDI**  
Watch closely, puppy detective.

**BENTLEY**  
I'm watching with my whole face.

Indi plants herself on the rock. Her purple skin ripples. Warm grey and gold patches bloom across her body. Her smooth skin lifts into tiny bumpy points, matching the rock texture.

Bentley's mouth falls open in puppy wonder.

**BENTLEY**  
Your skin did a costume change!

**INDI**  
Color and texture. Very fancy. Very useful.

Bentley steps closer and sniffs the rock.

**BENTLEY**  
Indi?

Indi is perfectly camouflaged except for one eye peeking open.

**INDI**  
No Indi here. Only extremely normal rock.

Bentley circles, tail-stub wiggling.

**BENTLEY**  
Hmm. This rock sounds smug.

Indi pops back to purple.

**INDI**  
That is because this rock is winning.

Bentley play-bows.

**BENTLEY**  
Again! Again! I can find you this time.

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**  
An octopus can change color and texture in a blink. Smooth to bumpy, purple to rock, here to... where did she go?

Indi vanishes into the rock pattern again.

Bentley spins the wrong direction.

**BENTLEY**  
I was looking at you, and then you became geology.

Indi giggles. A little shell slides out from behind the rock and taps Bentley's paw.

**BENTLEY**  
A clue!

**INDI**  
Or a distraction.

Bentley looks up. Indi is now on the other side of her, proud.

**BENTLEY**  
You are tiny trouble.

**INDI**  
Thank you.

## Scene Pod 3 — Why Hiding Matters

**Setting:** Same tidepool geography, moving slowly toward a crevice. The mood stays adventurous, not depressing.

**Production intent:** Introduce eel danger through Indi's reaction first. The eel must be in the same world as the characters, not a random cutaway.

Bentley and Indi explore together. Bentley steps carefully now, trying not to splash Indi.

**BENTLEY**  
So you hide because it is fun?

**INDI**  
Fun. Clever. Dramatic.

Indi climbs onto a smaller stone and does a little "ta-da" with two arms.

**INDI (CONT'D)**  
Also because some things in the tidepool have very big mouths.

Bentley looks around, interested rather than scared.

**BENTLEY**  
Like pelicans?

**INDI**  
Bigger underwater.

A dark ripple passes across the water. Indi's eyes widen. Her teal ring spots brighten.

Bentley notices Indi's face change.

**BENTLEY**  
Your spots just went flashlight.

**INDI**  
That means: do not be crunchy.

Bentley leans closer.

**BENTLEY**  
Are you crunchy?

**INDI**  
Not if I am doing my job.

From a shadowed gap, the eel silhouette glides past: not gory, not cute, just big enough to matter.

Indi flattens against the rock, matching its color and bumpiness.

Bentley instinctively steps in front of her.

**BENTLEY**  
I'll chase it away!

Indi pops one eye open, alarmed.

**INDI**  
Please do not chase anything with that many teeth.

Bentley stops mid-step.

**BENTLEY**  
Right. New plan. No chasing teeth.

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**  
A moray eel is not a villain. It is a hungry neighbor. For a small octopus, hiding is not being shy. It is being smart.

The eel shadow passes. Indi relaxes, color returning.

Bentley looks at Indi with new respect.

**BENTLEY**  
So hide-and-seek is survival.

Indi gives a tiny proud nod.

**INDI**  
And style.

## Scene Pod 4 — Stop. Look. Listen.

**Setting:** Same crevice pocket. A shell clue and Bentley's tag connect emotionally. Keep both characters visible.

**Production intent:** Bentley's lesson should be active and upbeat: she learns to pause so she can help better. Not sad, not defeated.

Indi reaches one arm toward Bentley's gold paw tag.

**INDI**  
What is that shiny paw?

**BENTLEY**  
My Listening Tag. It glows when I do the hardest thing in the world.

Indi leans in.

**INDI**  
Taxes?

**BENTLEY**  
Being still.

Indi touches the tag gently with one sucker. She makes a delighted face.

**INDI**  
It tastes like sunshine and wet dog.

Bentley beams.

**BENTLEY**  
That is my personal brand.

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**  
Octopus arms can taste and feel. When Indi touched Bentley's paw, she was not just holding on. She was reading a new friend.

Indi's arm curls around Bentley's paw. Bentley goes very still, but bright-eyed and excited, like this is a game she is proud to win.

The tag glows softly.

**BENTLEY**  
Stop. Look. Listen.

Indi watches the glow.

**INDI**  
You really can stop.

**BENTLEY**  
Only for important things.

**INDI**  
Am I important?

Bentley answers immediately.

**BENTLEY**  
Extremely.

Indi softens. Then, mischievous again, she changes just her head to Bentley's red-fawn color.

**INDI**  
Then I shall become extremely Bentley.

Bentley squeals.

**BENTLEY**  
You did me!

Indi turns more red-fawn, holding the color long enough for Bentley to react. Her teal eyes and ring spots stay stable.

**BENTLEY (CONT'D)**  
You're a teeny-tiny tidepool Bentley!

Indi struts in a tiny circle.

**INDI**  
Do I look brave?

**BENTLEY**  
You look like trouble wearing my outfit.

They laugh together.

## Scene Pod 5 — The Escape Artist

**Setting:** Same tidepool, just beyond the play spot. The eel shadow returns and pushes the scene into action.

**Production intent:** This is the big adventure beat. Keep Bentley and Indi connected. Show the squeeze clearly. No slow lag, no loop, no static wedge.

A shadow slides over the water again.

Indi's red-fawn play-color flashes back to purple. Her ring spots brighten.

**INDI**  
Big mouth. Back again.

Bentley plants her paws, ready but not reckless.

**BENTLEY**  
No chasing teeth.

Indi darts toward a narrow gap under a rock. She starts to camouflage, but her panic makes her color flicker. She wedges halfway through.

**INDI**  
Oh no. Too tight. Too tight!

Bentley rushes close and lifts a paw to pull.

**INDI (CONT'D)**  
No yanking! I am soft parts!

Bentley freezes. The tag glows.

**BENTLEY**  
Right. Soft parts. No yanking.

The eel shadow moves closer in the background.

Bentley lowers herself to Indi's eye level.

**BENTLEY (CONT'D)**  
You are the hide-and-seek champion. What does a champion do?

Indi breathes fast.

**INDI**  
Hide?

**BENTLEY**  
And?

Indi looks at the tiny gap.

**INDI**  
Squeeze.

**BENTLEY**  
Squeeze like soup.

Indi blinks.

**INDI**  
That is not science wording.

**BENTLEY**  
Squeeze like fancy soup.

Indi laughs despite herself. Her body relaxes.

**BENTLEY (CONT'D)**  
You don't have bones. You can pour yourself through. Slow breath. Soft body.

Indi closes her eyes. Her body goes fluid and slips through the coin-sized gap. One arm, then head, then the rest of her pours safely to the other side.

Bentley bounces in place.

**BENTLEY (CONT'D)**  
You did it! You fancy-souped!

Indi reforms on the far side, whole and proud.

**INDI**  
Escape artist.

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**  
The only hard part of an octopus is her tiny beak. If the opening is bigger than that, she can pour her whole body through.

The eel shadow fades away. Bentley and Indi look at each other across the rock gap, both relieved.

**BENTLEY**  
I wanted to help by pulling.

**INDI**  
You helped by listening.

Bentley's tag glows one more time, brighter.

---

## Scene Pod 6 — Three Hearts And The Next Clue

**Setting:** Calm tidepool after the adventure. Warm, playful, cozy. Then a curious hook toward the beach.

**Production intent:** The science button should be beautiful and character-led. No cheap floating hearts over a static face. Keep both characters alive and close.

Indi crawls back beside Bentley. They sit together in the shallow water.

Bentley watches Indi with full puppy admiration.

**BENTLEY**  
You changed color, tasted my tag, and turned into soup.

**INDI**  
Fancy soup.

**BENTLEY**  
You are amazing.

Indi tries to look casual. She fails adorably.

**INDI**  
I also have three hearts.

Bentley gasps.

**BENTLEY**  
Three? I only have one and it already gets very busy.

Indi places one arm over her own body, proud.

**INDI**  
Two help my gills. One sends blue blood everywhere.

**BENTLEY**  
Blue blood?

Indi nods.

**INDI**  
Very fashionable.

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**  
Three hearts. Blue blood. A body built for hiding, crawling, squeezing, and surprising a very curious puppy.

Bentley leans closer, joyful.

**BENTLEY**  
I'm glad I found your eyeball.

Indi smiles with her eyes.

**INDI**  
I am glad you stopped chasing it.

Indi reaches behind a rock and pulls out a small spiral shell. She presses it into Bentley's paw.

Bentley sits up, intrigued.

**BENTLEY**  
Another clue?

Indi points one arm toward the sand beyond the tidepool.

**INDI**  
The next mystery is not in the water.

Bentley and Indi turn together.

A perfect line of ants marches inland, carrying a crumb the size of a toy car.

Bentley's eyes go huge.

**BENTLEY**  
That crumb has legs.

**INDI**  
Lots of them.

**BENTLEY**  
Where are they going?

Indi leans in, mysterious.

**INDI**  
Underground.

Bentley's whole body vibrates with curiosity.

**BENTLEY**  
Indi?

**INDI**  
Yes?

**BENTLEY**  
Do underground crumbs need boops?

Indi thinks.

**INDI**  
Only after we listen.

Bentley grins, bright and ready.

**BENTLEY**  
Stop. Look. Listen.

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**  
And just like that, the tidepool handed Bentley one more secret.

**CARD:** NEXT TIME: THE ANT CITY BENEATH OUR FEET

## Shot Pod Requirements

Each pod should be generated/assembled as a mini-scene, not as isolated clips.

| Pod | Must-have coverage |
| --- | --- |
| 1 Rock Winked | Bentley boop approach; eye reveal; two-shot Indi/Bentley introduction; Bentley delighted reaction |
| 2 Hide-And-Seek | Two-shot game setup; Indi color+texture transformation; Bentley searching; Indi pop-out; shell clue |
| 3 Why Hiding Matters | Two-shot walking/exploring; Indi eyespots flare; eel shadow in same geography; Bentley protects but does not chase |
| 4 Stop Look Listen | Paw/tag touch; tag glow; Bentley/Indi two-shot; Indi turns red-fawn; playful reaction |
| 5 Escape Artist | Eel shadow return; Indi wedged; Bentley stops herself; squeeze through gap; triumphant two-shot |
| 6 Hearts And Clue | Cozy two-shot; three-hearts line with living Indi; shell handoff; shared look inland; ants reveal |

## Music Notes

Default palette: bright pizzicato/marimba/ukulele/glockenspiel with playful ocean percussion.

Do not use slow sad piano as the main bed. Eel moments can use short suspense stings, but the episode should quickly return to curiosity and play.

## QA Rules For This Script

- Audio-only pass must feel clear and fun.
- Muted animatic must show Bentley and Indi's relationship.
- Every speaking beat needs visible speaker ownership or obvious VO-over action.
- No static character shots.
- No human teeth or human-style smiles.
- No black-frame transitions.
- Kling 3.0 for new motion.
- Worst frame fails.
