## 0. THE SPINE *(sanity-check this in five seconds)*

- **Feeling:** *it's safe to be seen.* (A small thing that hides from everything finds one friend it doesn't have to hide from.)
- **Bentley's lesson:** *Stop. Look. Listen.* — you can't help something you're still chasing. Stillness is how you finally meet it.
- **Guest:** **INDI**, a shy California two-spot octopus. Want: to be seen / known. Fear: being seen gets her eaten. Her fear *mirrors and completes* Bentley's lesson.

**The 4 facts ARE the plot (not trivia stapled on):**
| Fact | How it IS the story |
|---|---|
| **Camouflage** — changes **color *and* texture** in a blink | This is *why Bentley can never catch her*, and *how Indi survives the eel*. The hide-and-seek IS the camouflage. |
| **The eel hunts her** (moray, real reef predator) | This is *why* Indi hides from everything — including, at first, from Bentley. The danger explains the loneliness. |
| **Boneless** — pours through a gap the size of her beak (~a coin) | This is *how she escapes the eel* at the climax — and what Bentley coaches her to use instead of yanking. |
| **Taste-by-touch** — ~2/3 of her neurons are in her arms | This is *how Indi learns to trust Bentley* — the paw-hold is her "reading" him; it's the bonding turn, not a fact-dump. |

**Cozy closing wonder (button only, no plot weight):** three hearts, copper-blue blood, biggest heart rests when she swims — *so she'd rather crawl up beside a friend and stay.* Earns the final image.

**Cold-open hook:** "the rock had an eyeball." **Cliffhanger:** a line of ants carries a crumb the size of a car → EP3, *The Ant City Beneath Our Feet.*

A 5-year-old can now answer: *"What was it about?"* → "a shy octopus who didn't have to hide from the puppy." *"What did you learn?"* → "stop and listen before you grab."

## 1. WHAT CHANGED FROM v9 (and why) — preserve-what-works

v9 fixed the black-dips, the loops, the dropped lines, and the mis-voiced octopus. Those wins are **kept**. v2 raises three things v9 left at "good enough," all of them *story/dialogue/shot* problems the owner still feels:

1. **Camouflage is now load-bearing, and it finally gets a real shot.** v9's camo science (`N_camo`) plays over a *still beauty shot* (`a1_08_camo`) that doesn't change color — so the single most important fact ("she changes color AND texture") is *told but never shown*, and the chase has no visible reason Bentley keeps missing. **v2 adds exactly one bridge — BRIDGE-CAMO — a true grey→gold / smooth→spiky change**, and re-times the chase so we *see* a "rock" become Indi and melt back. This is the biggest single upgrade.
2. **The two long narrator speeches stop sitting on one static face.** `N_taste` (13.8s) and `N_hearts` (22.2s) in v9 each play over a single near-static Indi/paw-hold hold — a top source of the "static / pale / wrong character" feeling. v2 **trims both speeches** and **spreads each across 2–3 living angles** (paw-CU → arm-traces-tag insert → two-shot), so the picture moves with the words and never stalls. `N_hearts` is cut from 22s of prose to a warm ~10s closer.
3. **The eel's danger is wired to *Indi* on a tight causal chain, and her camouflage pays it off.** v9 added BRIDGE-1 (Indi+eel in frame) — kept. v2 makes the *logic* explicit in dialogue and structure: Bentley corners Indi → Indi's only escape is to flee → she flees toward the lair → the eel is *there* → Bentley realizes *she* did it. Then in Act 4 the same eel returns and **Indi's camouflage + boneless body are literally what save her**, so the Act-1 facts cash out at the climax (they didn't, cleanly, before).

Plus smaller tightening: every line re-checked as "a reaction to the line before"; the banned dark line stays scrubbed; the bonding beat avoids `a3_20_twoshot` (red socks) entirely; the "three hearts" beat gets a real living two-shot instead of a graphic.

**Net:** ~5 VO labels change, **1 new bridge shot** (BRIDGE-CAMO) plus the already-built BRIDGE-1, and a re-sequenced cut. Everything else reuses existing footage.
