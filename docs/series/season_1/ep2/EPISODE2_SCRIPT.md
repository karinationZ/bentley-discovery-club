# THE OCTOPUS ESCAPE ARTIST
### *Bentley's Big Adventures* — Season 1 · Episode 2
**Read-through script for approval** · target runtime ~4:30

---

**CAST**
- **BENTLEY** — a young red-fawn Boxer pup. Big heart, bigger curiosity. Helps by *doing* (her flaw). *(voice: LauraZ)*
- **INDI** — a shy California two-spot octopus. Wants to disappear; hopes to be seen. *(voice: locked — guarded-but-warm)*
- **NARRATOR / "GRANDPA THEODORE"** — warm, twinkly storyteller. *(voice: Bill)*

**SETTING** — The reef-edge of Crystal Cove tide pool. Bright morning.

**RUNNING DEVICE** — Bentley's collar holds a small glowing gold paw-print **"Listening Tag."** When Bentley gets *still* and truly listens, it lights up. *(Stop. Look. Listen.)*

---

## COLD OPEN — "THE ROCK HAD AN EYEBALL" *(0:00–0:18)*

**EXT. CRYSTAL COVE TIDE POOL — MORNING.** Sparkling water, wet black rocks, jewel-bright anemones. BENTLEY pads up, nose down, little docked tail going. She spots one cute, speckled rock and gets *the look._

**BENTLEY** *(hushed, delighted)*
Helloooo, little rock. You look like you need… a boop.

*She reaches out one paw —*

*The "rock" SNAPS OPEN one enormous **EYE.** It flushes deep red — and **VANISHES** in a puff of ink.*

*Bentley sits in the settling ink cloud, blinking.*

**BENTLEY** *(through the ink)*
…The ROCK had an EYEBALL.

**SMASH TO TITLE — *BENTLEY'S BIG ADVENTURES.***

---

## ACT ONE — THE CHASE *(0:18–1:35)*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
That's Bentley. When Bentley meets a mystery, she has one big rule: don't just *watch* it — **catch** it. *(beat)* And that little glow on her collar? That's her Listening Tag. When Bentley holds still and *truly* listens, it lights up and the world whispers its secrets. Trouble is… Bentley's not very good at "still." Not yet.

*Bentley pounces from rock to rock — booping each speckled one.*

**BENTLEY**
Gotcha! …Nope. Gotcha! …Rock. Gotcha—!

*The sixth "rock" SQUIRTS a jet of water right in her face. She sputters.*

**BENTLEY** *(CONT'D)*
…Okay. That one was *rude.*

*A patch of rock beside her shimmers — and in the **blink of an eye** its skin shifts color AND goes from smooth to bumpy, melting into the stone.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
An octopus can change her color *and* her texture faster than you can blink — smooth to spiky, grey to gold — to vanish into rock, coral, or sand. The greatest hide-and-seek champion in the sea.

*Bentley spots a single curling purple arm. She LUNGES —*

*— and Indi is **gone** in a swirl of ink. Where she sat: a tiny, neatly arranged spiral **shell.** Bentley tilts her head at it, puzzled, then bounds off after the ink.*

---

## ACT TWO — IT GETS WORSE *(1:35–2:25)*

*Bentley corners a flash of purple in a tight rock **crevice.** INDI presses back, two iridescent-blue **eyespots** flaring bright with fear.*

**BENTLEY**
Hey— hey, it's okay, I just wanna *help*—

*Indi PANICS. A puff of ink — and she bolts… straight toward a dark, low **lair** in the reef.*

*From the shadows: a long, sinuous **silhouette** stirs. A glint of eye. A flash of teeth. A **moray eel.** Bentley freezes, ears down, the truth landing on her —*

**BENTLEY** *(CONT'D)* *(quiet, horrified)*
…I chased her *right at it.*

---

## ACT THREE — THE TURN *(2:25–3:25)*

*Bentley stops. Sits. **Backs away.** She closes her eyes and goes completely still — and the **Listening Tag glows.**_

**BENTLEY** *(eyes closed, going still — the Listening Tag glows; her catchphrase)*
Stop. Look. *Listen.*

*A long, long beat. The water settles. Then — slowly, **choosing** it — Indi creeps out of the dark, eyespots dimming soft.*

**INDI** *(barely a whisper)*
…You stopped.

**BENTLEY** *(gentle, not moving)*
Why do you keep hiding?

**INDI** *(proud, then softer)*
I'm not hiding — I'm the hide-and-seek **CHAMPION!** Eight arms, zero bones, completely undefeated! *(beat, smaller)* …You're the first one who's ever found me.

**BENTLEY** *(warm)*
Well, I'm not leaving. I'll just sit right here. Come out whenever you're ready… champ.

*The longest pause. Then a shy tentacle reaches out… and curls gently around Bentley's **paw.** It traces the paw, the tag, the fur.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
And here's a secret: an octopus can **taste by touch.** Nearly two-thirds of her thinking happens in her arms — so when Indi holds your paw, she's not just feeling you. She's *reading* you.

**INDI**
I wasn't running from *you.* I run from everybody. It's safer to disappear. *(beat)* But I kept leaving you those shells… because somebody finally needs to **listen.** The open water — it's gone wrong.

---

## ACT THREE-AND-A-HALF — THE COLOR GAME *(the bonding beat) (3:25–3:55)*

*The fear melts into mischief. Indi gives a shy little wiggle — and her purple skin **blushes**, patches of warm **red-fawn** blooming across her until she's… the exact color of Bentley's coat.*

**BENTLEY** *(gasps, overjoyed)*
You did— you did *ME!* You're a tiny ME!

*They play: Indi melts into a rock, pops back out two feet over; Bentley spins, delighted, nose-to-eyespot. Pure, giddy, no-plot joy.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)** *(warm chuckle)*
Two new friends. One can change into anything in the sea… and chose to look like a dog.

---

## ACT FOUR — THE ESCAPE ARTIST *(3:55–4:25)*

*A shadow falls. The **eel-silhouette** glides back. Indi flinches and darts for cover — and **WEDGES** tight in a too-small gap between two rocks. Stuck. Eyespots flaring._

*Bentley's instinct: she grabs to **YANK** her free —*

**INDI** *(panicked)*
No—! You'll hurt me—!

*Bentley stops herself. Breathes. Lowers her voice.*

**BENTLEY**
Okay. Okay. You don't need me to pull. *(gentle)* Remember what you are? You don't *have* bones. You can pour yourself out — just **breathe.**

*Indi closes her eyes. Her whole body goes soft… and **pours** — like water — through a gap no wider than a **coin**, to safety. She reforms, whole, on the far side.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
It's true — the only hard part of an octopus is her tiny beak. Everything else is boneless. Give her a hole bigger than that beak — about the size of a coin — and she can squeeze her whole self through it. The real escape artist.

---

## ACT FIVE — THE BUTTON *(4:25–4:45)*

*Indi, safe, looks up at Bentley — eyespots soft and warm now.*

**BENTLEY**
You don't have to disappear to be safe with me. I **see** you. *(beat)* And you're not scary, OR small. You're **amazing.**

**NARRATOR (V.O.)** *(tender)*
Three hearts. Blue blood — real blue, the copper kind. The biggest heart even takes a little rest when she swims… which is why this brave, boneless, color-changing wonder would rather *crawl* right up beside a friend, and stay.

---

## TAG / CLIFFHANGER → EPISODE 3 *(4:45–5:00)*

*Indi presses something urgent into Bentley's paw — another small **shell**, warm with meaning.*

**INDI** *(low, serious)*
There's a **gathering.** You have to see. Look — *inland.*

*She points one arm up the beach. Bentley turns. A **perfect line of ants** marches off across the sand… carrying a crumb the size of a **car.**_

**BENTLEY**
Wait— where are you all *GOING?*

*HARD CUT TO CARD: **"NEXT TIME: THE ANT CITY BENEATH OUR FEET."***

**BENTLEY** *(quiet, to herself — her promise; catchphrase)*
Stop. Look. *Listen.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
And the cove, little friends? It's keeping a *very* big secret.

### END OF EPISODE

Moray eel is a real bimac predator in this habitat — kept as a soft silhouette for ages 4–8.*
