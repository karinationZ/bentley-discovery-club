# The Mystery of the Talking Trees
### Season 1 · Episode 5 · Bentley's Big Adventures

> **Logline:** Spooky whispers haunt the forest at night. Bentley goes ghost-hunting — and discovers the trees really *are* talking, through a secret underground network, and a lonely little sapling who feels invisible has been connected to everyone the whole time.

**Habitat:** The coastal forest grove · **Power used:** Listening (+ a shrink down to the roots) · **Guest:** **Sprout**, a young sapling in the shade of giants.
**The kid-feeling we're really telling (SEL):** *Feeling lonely, unseen, and disconnected — and discovering you were never actually alone.*
**Bentley's arc:** Hears whispers, assumes a *monster*, tries to "fight/find the voice" (doing) → learns the forest talks *underground* → has to get quiet and listen *below* to hear the truth.

## The 3 Dramatized Facts (real)
1. Trees are linked **underground by fungal threads (mycorrhizae)** — the "**Wood Wide Web**" — sharing water, food, and messages root-to-root.
2. Trees **warn each other of danger** — when bugs attack, a tree releases chemical signals so its neighbors raise their defenses *before* the bugs arrive.
3. Big "**mother trees**" feed sugar to small shaded seedlings through the network, keeping the next generation alive.

## Cold Hook (0:00–0:12)
Night. Fog. A low voice with no mouth: *"Bennn-tleyyy..."* The Boxer freezes mid-step, one paw up. **BENTLEY:** *(tiny whisper)* "Okay. Which one of you trees just said my name. I will find you."

## The Bentley Method
- **Flaw on display:** Convinced it's a forest ghost, Bentley charges around "hunting the voice," barking at bushes, digging random holes, accusing an owl. (Comedy: she challenges a perfectly innocent log to a stare-down.)
- **Discovery + it gets worse:** Her digging slices through delicate root threads — and the whole grove's whispering goes *silent.* She's accidentally cut the forest's phone lines, and a sick patch of trees stops getting help.
- **THE TURN:** Stop. Look. Listen — *with your roots.* Shell glows. Bentley presses her ear to the earth and hears it: thousands of voices, and under them all, Sprout: *"They talk to everyone but me. I'm too little and too shaded. I don't think anyone even knows I'm here."*
- **Climax — clock + setback:** A beetle swarm targets the weakened, disconnected trees overnight. Bentley's surface fix (shooing beetles) fails — too many. Setback → she shrinks down and re-splices the broken network thread so the *mother tree's* warning and sugar can flow again; the grove raises its defenses *together* and shares strength with Sprout.
- **Emotional button:** "You were never alone, Sprout. You're connected to every tree in this whole forest. You just had to listen with your *roots.*"

## Signature Scene (dialogue)
> **SPROUT:** "Nobody talks to me. I'm down here in the dark."
> **BENTLEY:** "Put your roots out. Just a little. ...Now — feel that?"
> **SPROUT:** "...Something's *humming.* It's warm."
> **BENTLEY:** "That's the whole forest. That big tree up there? She's been sending you food this entire time. You couldn't see it. But you were never, ever on your own."

## Cliffhanger → Episode 6
Through the root-network, a distant tremor — water, a shadow, fear rippling from the coast. *"Something big is in the shallows. Everyone's scared of it."* The shell pulls Bentley toward the sea. → *The Shark Who Was Afraid.*
