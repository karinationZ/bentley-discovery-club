# The Secret Life of Bees
### Season 1 · Episode 9 · Bentley's Discovery Club

> **Logline:** Bentley shrinks into a buzzing golden city and meets Buzz, a young worker bee who thinks her job is boring and dreams of being the "important" one — right as the hive is about to starve and the only thing that can save it is the very dance Buzz keeps ignoring.

**Habitat:** Inside a beehive + a coastal wildflower meadow · **Power used:** Listening + shrinking · **Guest:** **Buzz**, a young worker bee.
**The kid-feeling we're really telling (SEL):** *Wanting to be the special "star," and not seeing the value of your own small, everyday role.*
**Bentley's arc:** Tries to make Buzz "important" by skipping her boring job / sneaking her toward the queen (doing — disrupts the hive) → learns every job runs the world → finally *reads the dance* she dismissed.

## The 3 Dramatized Facts (real)
1. Bees do a **waggle dance** to tell each other *exactly* where flowers are — the angle points the direction, and the length says the distance. They give **directions by dancing.**
2. A hive is a **superorganism** where the queen just lays eggs — she doesn't boss anyone; the **workers run everything**, and each bee changes jobs as she grows (nurse → builder → guard → forager).
3. Bees see **ultraviolet patterns** on flowers — secret glowing "landing-pad bullseyes" invisible to us — and bees **pollinate** the plants behind roughly **1 in 3 bites of our food.**

## Cold Hook (0:00–0:12)
Shrunk Bentley tumbles into a glowing city of golden hexagons, wings roaring around her. A bee zips up to her face: *"New girl! You're LATE for the dance!"* **BENTLEY:** "...the *WHAT* now?"

## The Bentley Method
- **Flaw on display:** Bentley decides Buzz feels small because she has a "boring" job, so she helps her *cut the line to meet the queen* and "be important." The hive's rhythm stumbles; guards get cross. (Comedy: Bentley keeps loudly calling the queen "Your Majesty, the Boss" and getting corrected — the queen doesn't *rule* anything.)
- **Discovery + it gets worse:** A storm flattened the nearby flowers. The hive is running out of food, and Bentley's meddling pulled foragers off the job. Now nobody knows where the last far-off flower field is.
- **THE TURN:** Stop. Look. Listen. Shell glows. Bentley finally *watches the waggle dance* she'd ignored — and hears Buzz: *"I thought my job didn't matter. I'm just a forager. I'm not the special one."*
- **Climax — clock + setback:** The hive must reach the distant meadow before dark or starve. Bentley's confident "I'll lead, follow me!" sends the swarm the wrong way (she misread the dance angle). Setback → she humbly lets *Buzz* read and perform the dance correctly; the foragers follow Buzz to the hidden UV-bright field and save the hive.
- **Emotional button:** "You're not 'just' a little bee with a small job, Buzz. Every flower you touch feeds the whole world. The world runs on the small jobs — and on the ones brave enough to do them."

## Signature Scene (dialogue)
> **BUZZ:** "The queen's important. The guards are important. I just... fly to flowers."
> **BENTLEY:** "Buzz. One in three bites of food *everyone* eats — that's *you.* You're not at the bottom of this. You're holding the whole thing up and you didn't even know it."
> **BUZZ:** "...Me?"
> **BENTLEY:** "You. Now show me that dance — for real this time. The hive's counting on the 'boring' job tonight."

## Cliffhanger → Episode 10 (FINALE)
The hive is saved — but Buzz reveals *why* the flowers failed: the trouble is blowing in off the sea, where the water's gone wrong. Suddenly **every shell on the coast glows at once.** Indi's voice, Finn's, Mara's, Stella's — all of them: *"Bentley. The ocean needs the Club. All of it. Now."* → *The Great Ocean Rescue.*
