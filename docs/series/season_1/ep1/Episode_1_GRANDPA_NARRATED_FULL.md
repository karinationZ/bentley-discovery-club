# Episode 1 — "The Voice in the Shell" · GRANDPA-NARRATED FULL EPISODE
### One complete episode, start to finish · ~4–5 min · narrator-led (no lip-sync needed)
> **Format:** Theodore (warm Nat-Geo grandpa) narrates the whole adventure + the science with wonder. Bentley & Pip **act and react** — expressions, body language — they do NOT talk on screen (a few non-verbal sounds only). This fixes the mouth/lip-sync problem, puts the grandpa voice front-and-center, and is a beloved kids'-nature format.
> **The real fact (the spine):** a hermit crab is born soft and homeless, borrows a shell, must trade up as it grows — and the whole tide pool trades homes at once in a **vacancy chain.**
> **Characters:** Bentley-MASTER `1c82ee5b` · Pip-clean `752fec82` · Tidepool-cove `d406065e`. Reuse the on-model clips already built where noted.

## ACT 1 — THE VOICE (hook)
**N1** ♻️ glowing shell / dawn pool — *"Every morning, the tide slips away from Crystal Cove… and leaves behind a whole world of secrets."*
**N2** ♻️ Bentley sniff / ears-up discover — *"This is Bentley. And this morning, Bentley heard something nobody else could — a tiny voice, calling for help."*
**N3** ♻️ follow-voice → reveal Pip in cracked shell — *"It wasn't a fish. It wasn't a bird. It was a little crab named Pip — stuck, and very, very worried."*

## ACT 2 — THE SECRET OF THE SHELL 🔬
**N4** ♻️ Pip CU (cracked shell) — *"Now, Pip is a hermit crab. And hermit crabs are born without a home of their own."*
**N5** ♻️ soft-body lift/tuck — *"Their bodies are soft — so they borrow an empty seashell to stay safe. The shell isn't part of Pip… it's his house. And he carries it everywhere."* 🔬
**N6** ♻️ stranded / sun-insert — *"But Pip had grown too big. His little shell had cracked. And out on the dry rock, in the rising sun, a soft little crab is in real danger."* 🔬

## ACT 3 — THE WRONG RESCUE
**N7** ♻️ Bentley gasp / eager — *"Bentley LOVES to help. So she did the very first thing she thought of…"*
**N8** ♻️ nudge toward water — *"…she tried to put him back in the sea."*
**N9** ♻️ not-a-swimmer flip (recoil + foam) — *"But a hermit crab is no swimmer! The crashing waves would sweep tiny Pip away. Bentley had it all wrong."*
**N10** ♻️ two-shot / set-down — *"Pip didn't need the ocean. He needed something much harder to find… a brand-new home that fit just right."*

## ACT 4 — ⭐ THE WONDER: THE VACANCY CHAIN 🔬
**N11** ♻️ deflated Pip / Bentley stops-looks-listens — *"But where does a crab find a new house? You can't buy one. You can't build one. So Bentley stopped… she looked… and she listened."*
**N12** 🆕 hermit crabs emerging across the rocks — *"And that's when the most amazing thing happened. From all across the tide pool… the hermit crabs came."*
**N13** 🆕 crabs lined up biggest-to-smallest — *"They lined up — biggest to smallest. Because hermit crabs share a secret."* 🔬
**N14** 🆕 a big new shell appears; biggest crab moves in — *"When a brand-new shell appears, the biggest crab moves in…"* 🔬
**N15** 🆕 cascade swap down the line (click-click-click) — *"…and leaves its old shell for the next crab — who leaves ITS shell for the next — all the way down the line! Click… click… click!"* 🔬⭐
**N16** 🆕 the chain reaches Pip; perfect-fit shell (Bentley's glowing shell) — *"Scientists call it a vacancy chain — a whole neighborhood, trading homes all at once. And it's real. The very last shell… fit Pip perfectly."* 🔬⭐

## ACT 5 — HOME + DISCOVERY TAG
**N17** 🆕 wave carries Pip (new shell) to the cool pool, Bentley happy — *"Safe at last — in a home of his very own."*
**N18** 🆕 "Today we discovered" recap visuals — *"Today we discovered: a hermit crab borrows its home… it trades up as it grows… and when the time comes, the whole tide pool helps — in an amazing vacancy chain."* 🔬
**N19** ♻️ Bentley content / to camera — *"Stop. Look. Listen… and you'll find the wonder, everywhere."*

## ACT 6 — CLIFFHANGER
**N20** 🆕 purple octopus tentacle rises offshore — *"But out past the rocks… something purple was watching. And THAT… is a story for next time."*
