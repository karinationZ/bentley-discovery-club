## Pacing / timing
- Front-loaded: by **0:08** we've met Pip and seen the cracked shell; by **0:25** the not-a-swimmer flip has fired and the mystery is locked. The back half is the fact + the quest hook.
- The scary beat (Shot 7) is **fast** — terror → freeze → he's safe on the sand within ~2s, warm music never drops out. Urgent, not traumatic.

## The two ⭐ science visuals (note #2)
- **Shot 9 (the anchor):** Pip lifts up just enough to show his soft, curled hind-body (cute, not gross), then tucks it back into the spiral shell with a snug little "click." Read instantly: *soft animal + borrowed protective home.* This is the shot we'll give the most keyframe takes.
- **Shot 7 + 2 + 10:** the cracked, too-small shell is shown repeatedly so "it doesn't fit anymore" is unmistakable.
