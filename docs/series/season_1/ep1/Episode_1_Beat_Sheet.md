# Episode 1: "The Voice in the Shell" — BEAT SHEET
### Series premiere · target runtime ~7:45 · the episode that teaches the audience what this show IS

> **Spine:** A scared hermit crab is stranded and *growing* — and the only way to save him is the astonishing real **hermit-crab vacancy chain.** The science isn't mentioned; it's the mystery, the obstacle, and the rescue. **Remove the science and the plot collapses.**
> **Bentley's arc:** "I help by grabbing" → "I help by *listening*."
> **Narration:** light warm narrator (≈6–8 lines: open, 2–3 wonder-frames, close). All emotion carried by Bentley, Pip, Stella.
> **Method/catchphrase:** *"Stop. Look. Listen."* → rally *"Let's go find the WONDER!"*
> **Locked Elements:** `Bentley-MASTER` · `Pip` · `Stella` · `Tidepool-cove`.

Legend: 🔬 science · ❤️ emotion · 📈 retention · ⚡ Shorts · 🎬 elements

---

## SCENE 1 — COLD OPEN: the voice (0:00–0:30)
- **0:00** BLACK. A tiny trembling voice: *"…Help? Somebody?"*
- **Action:** SMASH to a macro of a **pale spiral shell glowing soft blue** in wet sand, a wave hissing past. Bentley's big wet nose shoves in, sniffs (real-dog sniff). She pulls back, ears perk → head-tilt → eyes widen.
- **Key line — BENTLEY:** *"Did the beach just… *talk* to me?"*
- 📈 **Hook:** opens on mystery + a question, never scenery. Who's calling for help?
- ⚡ **Short A:** "Did the beach just TALK to me?!"
- 🎬 `Bentley-MASTER` + `Tidepool-cove` + glowing shell.

## SCENE 2 — TITLE + RALLY + WONDER FLASH (0:30–0:52)
- **Action:** 2-note motif, logo sting. Bentley does her wiggle-butt. Then a **2-second "wonder flash"** — a quick dazzle of the tide pool's astonishing life (a sea star, an anemone blooming, the glittering pool) so even the hook says *"this is a discovery show."*
- **Key line — BENTLEY:** *"Stop. Look. Listen… let's go find the WONDER!"*
- 📈 Branded, fast, musical. The wonder-flash is the fix for "feels like just a cute dog."
- 🎬 `Bentley-MASTER` + `Tidepool-cove`.

## SCENE 3 — THE FLAW: helping the wrong way (0:52–2:05)
- **Action:** Bentley finds **Pip** — a tiny hermit crab stranded high on a sun-baked rock, panicking. Sure he "fell out of the ocean," she scoops him in her mouth and **gallops toward the crashing surf to put him "back."**
- **Key lines:** PIP (muffled): *"NO — not the big water, I'm too LITTLE—!"* → a wave looms, Bentley skids, drops him. PIP (furious): *"You almost fed me to the OCEAN!"* → BENTLEY (crushed): *"…I was helping."*
- ❤️ **Flaw established:** she helps by *grabbing and doing* — and gets it wrong. Plant: *his problem isn't the water.*
- 📈 Comedy + a "she made it worse" mini-cliffhanger.
- ⚡ **Short B:** "The worst rescue ever 😅"
- 🎬 `Bentley-MASTER` + `Pip` + `Tidepool-cove`.

## SCENE 4 — THE MYSTERY NAMED · 🔬 SCIENCE 1 (2:05–3:05)
- **Action:** **Stella** the sea star (one arm regrowing) drifts up. She names the real problem.
- **Key lines — STELLA:** *"He's not from the ocean, sweetheart. He's from *here.*"* … *"These pools are the ocean's *leftovers* — left behind when the tide goes out."* … *"And Pip? Pip's a hermit crab who's grown too big for his borrowed home."*
- 🔬 **Science 1 (revealed as the diagnosis):** a hermit crab is **born without a shell** — it **borrows empty snail shells and must trade up** as it grows. NARRATOR wonder-frame: *"Did you know a hermit crab is born… without a home?"*
- 🔬 **The clock:** the sun is climbing, the rock is drying — a stranded tide-pool animal can **overheat and dry out.** (This *is* the stakes, not a footnote.)
- ❤️ Bentley realizes her "rescue" was based on not understanding him.
- 🎬 `Bentley-MASTER` + `Stella` + `Pip` + `Tidepool-cove`.

## SCENE 5 — IT GETS WORSE · science shown by failure (3:05–4:05)
- **Action:** Determined to fix it *fast*, Bentley jams Pip into a big empty shell — he **rattles around, exposed.** Then a too-small one — he can't fit. Comedy: she mistakes a **sea anemone for a flower** and it eats her snack. Her panicked zoomies knock pebbles, wedging Pip worse. Sun blazes.
- 🔬 **Science (shown, not told):** the **fit is survival** — wrong shell = exposed to predators + can't hold water. 🔬 **Bonus wonder:** *anemones are animals, not plants.*
- **Key line — STELLA (dry):** *"That's not a flower. That's an *animal.* And it *will* eat your snack."*
- 📈 Escalation — every fix makes it worse; the clock tightens. ⚡ **Short C:** "I fed my snack to a FLOWER (it was not a flower) 🌸"
- 🎬 all four elements.

## SCENE 6 — THE TURN: Stop. Look. Listen. (4:05–4:50)
- **Action:** Bentley spirals, reaches to grab Pip again — STELLA: *"Bentley. **Stop.**"* → **music cuts to silence.** Bentley sits, breathes; her collar shell **glows** — and she finally *hears* him.
- **Key lines — PIP (quiet, real):** *"I'm not scared of being stuck. I'm scared 'cause I'm *growing.* Nothing fits anymore… and nobody will help me move."* → BENTLEY (gentle): *"You're not too small, Pip. You're just *between shells.* …I know that feeling."*
- ❤️ **The heart:** emotional + scientific truth fused — outgrowing your home, feeling unheard. The arc pivots from *doing* to *listening*.
- 📈 The silence-drop is the episode's emotional spike. ⚡ **Short D:** "You're not too small — you're just between shells 🐚"
- 🎬 `Bentley-MASTER` + `Pip` + glowing shell.

## SCENE 7 — ⭐ THE WOW: THE VACANCY CHAIN · 🔬 SCIENCE 2 (4:50–6:20)
- **Action:** Bentley understands — she can't find *one* shell; she needs the **whole neighborhood to trade up together.** *"Big Adventures — let's go find the WONDER!"* She rallies the tide pool. The crabs **gather and line up by size**; a big new shell appears; the biggest moves in → each crab **cascades into the next-vacated shell** — *click, click, click* — a breathtaking chain of homes.
- 🔬 **Science 2 (the WOW reveal = the climax = the rescue):** the real **hermit-crab vacancy chain.** Bentley's jaw drops; NARRATOR: *"They line up by size and trade homes all at once. It's called a *vacancy chain* — and it's real."* Music swells — **the science is the spectacle.**
- ❤️📈 **SETBACK (the required dip):** the chain reaches Pip — the last shell is **one size too small.** Everyone freezes. Sun brutal. → Bentley *stops, listens,* and realizes: **her own glowing collar shell is exactly Pip's size.** *"It was on me the whole time."* She gives it to him — **perfect fit.**
- ⚡ **Short E:** "REAL hermit crabs trade houses in a CHAIN 🦀" (the showstopper fact)
- 🎬 `Bentley-MASTER` + `Pip` + crab crowd + `Stella` + `Tidepool-cove`.

## SCENE 8 — RESCUE + EMOTIONAL BUTTON (6:20–7:05)
- **Action:** A fresh wave rolls in and **carries Pip — in his glowing new shell — to the cool pool, safe, beating the sun.**
- **Key lines:** PIP: *"You gave me your shell."* → BENTLEY: *"You needed it more. Turns out I don't help by *grabbing.* I help by *listening.*"* → PIP (quiet): *"…You're the first one who actually did."*
- **Action:** Stella clips a **new glowing spiral shell** onto Bentley's collar — Big Adventures induction.
- ❤️ Arc complete; the lesson *lands because she changed.*
- 🎬 all elements.

## SCENE 9 — 🔬 DISCOVERY TAG (the identity stamp) (7:05–7:25)
- **Action:** A bright 10-second **"Today we discovered…"** recap: ① hermit crabs *borrow* their homes, ② they grow and *trade up*, ③ the amazing *vacancy chain.* Warm, wonder-filled — this is the beat that tells parents *this is a real science show.*
- **Key line — BENTLEY (to camera):** *"Stop. Look. Listen… and find the wonder."*
- 📈 Reinforces learning + brand; great for the description's "Discovery Notes."

## SCENE 10 — CLIFFHANGER + CTA (7:25–7:45)
- **Action:** Bentley trots up the beach, content. Her new shell **glows** and pulls her gaze out to sea — a **purple tentacle** rises and *waves.*
- **Key line — BENTLEY (head-tilt):** *"…Do octopuses usually say *hello?*"*
- CUT TO BLACK · 2-note motif · *"Episode 2 — The Octopus Escape Artist."* · kid-friendly **Subscribe** CTA.
- 🎬 `Bentley-MASTER` + `Tidepool-cove` + tentacle.
