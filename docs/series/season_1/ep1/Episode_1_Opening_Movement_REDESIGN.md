# Episode 1 — OPENING MOVEMENT (Redesign Spec)
### "The Crab Who Couldn't Go Home" · replaces old Scenes 3–4 · ~75–90s
> Approved structure (Approach A, curiosity-led), 2026-06-16. The fact is the engine: it creates the problem, drives the scene, and is delivered by action — never by narration. Bentley investigates but **rushes** (her flaw is intact); Pip is **scared, not angry**; the movement ends on **curiosity + a quest**, never on a crushed/crying beat.

## 1. Why this redesign exists
The old Scene 3 was "random action" — a dog grabs a crab and runs at the ocean, with the science deferred to a later narrated explainer. This movement rebuilds the episode's opening so it *is* the show's DNA: a **National-Geographic fact turned into an emotional mystery-adventure.** Curiosity drives it; the crab is the doorway into the fact.

## 2. The spine
- **Through-line from the cold open:** Bentley heard a voice and went to "find the wonder." This movement is the *payoff* — she follows the voice to its source. No jump cut.
- **Her objective:** not "rescue a crab" — **"figure out what's actually wrong with this little guy."** (She still can't help until she understands — but she doesn't know that yet; she rushes.)
- **The driving kid-question:** *"What's wrong with this crab — and why can't I just put him back in the ocean?"*
- **The hook out (the quest):** *"So where does a crab even GET a new house that fits?"* → pays off in the **vacancy-chain** WOW later.

## 3. Science accuracy (LOCKED — every line must respect this)
A tide-pool / intertidal hermit crab is **marine** and will **NOT "drown" in seawater.** Do not teach that. The real, accurate facts this movement teaches:
1. A hermit crab has a **soft, unprotected abdomen** — it **borrows an empty snail shell to protect its soft body** (from predators and from drying out).
2. It is **not a strong swimmer** and lives in the **calm tide pool / intertidal zone, not the crashing surf.** The open surf would **sweep it away, smash it on rocks, and expose it to predators.**
3. It does **not grow its own shell** — it must **find a bigger borrowed shell and trade up as it grows.**
4. Stranded in the open sun, an intertidal animal **dries out** — so the *right-fitting shell* (and getting back to the pool) is survival.
> Wrong (do NOT use): "I'll drown in the ocean." Right: "The surf will sweep me away / smash me / my soft body needs the shell."

## 4. Characters in this movement
- **Bentley** — eager helper whose flaw is that she **rushes to help before she understands.** She *says* her motto "Stop. Look. Listen." but doesn't actually practice it yet (that mastery is the episode's payoff). Curious, warm, a little embarrassed when wrong — **never devastated.**
- **Pip** — tiny, **scared and defensive, NOT randomly angry.** His prickliness is fear of being grabbed/dunked; it softens as Bentley finally listens. He is the **doorway** — the fact comes from him + Bentley's mistake.
- **Stella — OUT of this movement.** No mentor explainer here; the fact is earned through the action. (She can enter later in the episode.)

## 5. Beat-by-beat (~75–90s)

### Part 1 — The Discovery (curiosity)
1. **Follow the voice.** Bentley tracks the tiny voice across the rocks, ears swivelling. *PIP (O.S.): "…hello? is somebody there?"* The wonder-shimmer pulls her toward a hollow. *(Direct bridge from the cold open.)*
2. **Meet Pip.** Not the shiny magic thing she pictured — a tiny, real, scared crab wedged in a **dull, cracked** shell on a drying rock. *PIP: "Wh–who are you?! Don't squish me!"*
3. **Rushes the diagnosis (the flaw).** She's curious, starts to look, even says her motto — but is too eager to actually do it. Spots the crack and leaps: *BENTLEY: "Stop, look, listen — yep! You're a little lost sea creature, stuck on land!"*
4. **Pip tries to correct her.** *PIP: "What— no, I'm not lost, it's my shell, it's too—"* — but she's already bustling, sure she's got it. *(Plants the real problem AND her not-listening flaw.)*

### Part 2 — The Flip (the fact, delivered by the mistake)
5. **The helpful-wrong fix.** *BENTLEY: "Don't worry — I'll pop you right back in the water where you belong!"*
6. **⭐ NOT A SWIMMER (the flip).** She nudges him toward the surf — Pip erupts in **terror, not anger**: *PIP: "NO — not the big surf! I don't swim like a fish — it'll sweep me away and smash me on the rocks! My shell's the only thing keeping my soft body safe — STOP!"* Bentley freezes, pulls back. *(The assumption inverts; the fact lands through the scare. This is the first time she actually stops.)*
7. **"Wait, WHAT?"** Bentley, stunned and finally still, models the audience's question: *BENTLEY: "You live right by the water… but the big waves could sweep you away? And your shell isn't even PART of you?"* *PIP: "My body's soft — I borrow a shell to keep it safe. I find one that fits, and when I grow too big, I have to find a bigger one."* *(The "whoa" fact — earned, spoken by the crab.)*

### Part 3 — The Quest (hook into the vacancy chain)
8. **The real problem.** Bentley sees the cracked, too-small shell with new eyes: *BENTLEY: "So your house is broken AND too small… and without one that fits, you're not safe out here."* *PIP (deflated, glancing at the drying rock/sun): "I've looked everywhere. Nothing fits. And the longer I'm stuck in the open…"* *(Light stakes — exposed/drying/predators, not mortal peril.)*
9. **The hook.** Embarrassment → determination + wonder. *BENTLEY: "So where does a crab even GET a new house that fits?"* *PIP: "…That's the complicated part."* *BENTLEY (grinning): "Complicated's just a wonder we haven't figured out yet. Come on — let's find you a home."*
10. **Button.** A glimpse of the wider tide pool — other shells, other crabs — teasing the vacancy chain. They set off together.

## 6. How it satisfies the brief
- **Mystery Bentley solves:** what's wrong with Pip / why can't he go in the ocean. ✓
- **Fact taught:** soft body → borrowed shell → must trade up (accurate). ✓
- **Why Bentley cares:** she's a helper; the voice called for help. ✓
- **Why the audience cares:** Pip is scared, vulnerable, and *outgrowing his home* — emotionally true for a kid. ✓
- **Crab as doorway:** the fact comes from Pip + the mistake, not narration. ✓
- **Fact creates the problem, drives the scene, hooks the next:** ✓ → launches the vacancy-chain WOW.
