# The Butterfly Highway
### Season 1 · Episode 4 · Bentley's Big Adventures
**Rewrite 2026-07-03** (owner brief: female butterfly, playful/curious/adventure-driven, no drama or sad
beats, clever wholesome humor, discovery over conflict, fun interactions over long talks). Supersedes the
earlier SEL-drama draft (scared "Milo" / fear-of-the-journey). Written FOR the 10 mandatory production
rules (`episode-production` §0.0) + the Production Playbook.

> **Logline:** The sky turns into a river of orange when the great monarch migration pours over Crystal
> Cove — and Bentley talks her way into the world's best road-trip with **Mira**, a giggly first-time
> monarch who is having the time of her life, discovering that the trick to the biggest adventure is
> *not* having a map: you just follow the sun and the next flower.

**Habitat:** Coastal bluff → wildflower meadow → an old eucalyptus grove (the western-monarch migration
route down the California coast) · **Guest:** **MIRA the Monarch** (she/her) — bright, playful, fearless,
mischievous; the joy of the journey in butterfly form · **Tone:** light, funny, wondrous — ZERO peril
(the only "danger" is Bentley vs. a flower bank).

## 1 · THE SPINE

- **One feeling:** *The best adventures are the ones you don't have to figure out all at once — you just
  follow the fun, one flower at a time.*
- **One lesson (Bentley):** She wants to *plan it, lead it, and go fast* (her "doing" flaw — here played
  for pure comedy, never danger). She learns that stopping to *notice* turns a frantic race into the best
  day of her life.
- **Mira (the guest):** the opposite of a scared traveler — she's MORE game than Bentley, and her joyful
  "I don't know where I'm going and it's GREAT" attitude *is* the lesson. She's not lost; she's delighted.
- **The 4 facts (each a beat, not trivia):**
  1. Butterflies **taste with their feet** → first interaction + Bentley's copy-cat sneeze gag.
  2. Monarchs **migrate thousands of miles and cluster by the thousands in coastal groves** → the
     destination + the grove reveal.
  3. **No single butterfly knows the whole route — it takes several generations; they navigate by the
     sun** → reframed 100% positive as the joyful thesis (the wonder that nobody knows the whole way).
  4. **Metamorphosis** (caterpillar → chrysalis → wings) → the cozy button ("You were a *WORM?*").

**Child test:** "What was it about?" → *following the fun with a new friend.* "What did you learn?" →
*you don't have to know the whole way — just the next flower.*

**Design insight:** a butterfly has **no readable mouth**, so *every Mira line is lip-sync-safe by nature*
(VO-over-alive, no sync tool, no wrong-mouth risk — Rule 9 satisfied for free). Bentley stays **grounded**
the whole episode except **one** glide on a thermal — honoring the "flew with butterflies" hook while
avoiding the flying-consistency drift.

## SCIENCE CHECKED
**Monarch butterfly (*Danaus plexippus*), western/California population.**
1. Taste-with-feet (tarsal chemoreceptors). 2. Migration + coastal overwintering groves (thousands cluster
in California eucalyptus/pine groves). 3. Multi-generational route + time-compensated sun-compass
navigation (kept vague-but-true: "my kids and their kids"). 4. Complete metamorphosis
(egg→caterpillar→chrysalis→butterfly). **No predators, no peril.**
