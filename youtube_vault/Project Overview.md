# Project Overview

## Vision
**Bentley's Big Adventures** is a premium, Pixar-quality animated YouTube series where **Bentley** — a curious red-fawn Boxer puppy — explores the natural world and meets the creatures who live in it. Each episode turns one real piece of science into a warm, funny, emotionally complete little story. The aim is a show kids beg to rewatch and parents are glad to leave on: *Bluey's heart meets Nat-Geo's wonder.*

> [!quote] The emotional spine
> Bentley helps a creature with a problem, and in doing so teaches a gentle life lesson *alongside* the science. Episode 1's lesson: **"you don't find your home alone."**

## Target audience
- **Primary:** kids **ages 3–8** (preschool → early elementary).
- **Secondary:** the **parents/caregivers** co-viewing, and **teachers** wanting classroom-safe science.
- **Platform:** YouTube, set **"Made for Kids"** (COPPA).

> [!info] What "Made for Kids" changes
> It disables **comments, end screens, info cards, and notifications**. Engagement therefore lives in the **content and the description** — never rely on interactive elements (e.g. a "tap to dive in" end card won't exist; put next-episode links in the description text instead).

## Educational goals
- Teach **one clear, real, verified** science concept per episode — see [[National Geographic Standards]].
- Make it **emotionally memorable** by attaching the concept to a character's problem.
- Always land a **takeaway** a 5-year-old can repeat at dinner.

## The Bentley universe
| Character | Role | Canon |
|---|---|---|
| **Bentley** | Star — curious, kind, brave red-fawn Boxer puppy (based on the owner's real dog). | [[Bentley Master Prop]] |
| **Pip** | Ep 1 friend — a worried little hermit crab learning to grow up. | [[Pip the Hermit Crab]] |
| **Theodore** | Warm narrator (voice "Bill") who frames the science. | [[Voice Rules]] |
| *Octopus* | Recurring "new friend" button; Ep 2 lead. | *(to be locked)* |

**Setting — Crystal Cove:** a tide-pool world of wet black volcanic rock and glassy pools at golden dawn. Consistent across every shot, every episode.

## Season planning approach
- **Format:** standalone ~4–5 minute episodes, each built on **one creature + one concept**.
- **Reuse-first:** every episode reuses Bentley, the locked voices, the Crystal Cove environment, and the music/SFX library — only new *guest creatures* and *scene actions* are generated. See [[Token Efficiency Rules]].
- **Running threads:** small recurring gags/characters (e.g. the octopus "new friend") open loops that pay off later, keeping older kids engaged.
- **Per-episode pipeline:** script → shot list → keyframes (QA) → motion/talking renders → VO → assemble → mix → grade → QA → publish. See [[Production Workflow]].

> [!tip] Picking episodes
> Each episode = one creature with a genuinely surprising *true* behavior that creates a real "wow": hermit-crab **vacancy chain** (Ep 1 ✅), octopus **escape/camouflage** (Ep 2), and so on.
