## Logline
Bentley hears a tiny voice coming from a seashell and meets **Pip**, a worried hermit crab who's outgrowing his home — and discovers the secret of the **vacancy chain**.

## The science — the "wow"
**Vacancy chain:** when a hermit crab outgrows its shell, a whole *line* of crabs trades up at once — one new empty shell rehomes the entire tide pool. ([[National Geographic Standards]])
