# 🐾 Bentley's Big Adventures — Production Bible

> [!abstract] What this vault is
> The single source of truth for producing **Bentley's Big Adventures** — a premium YouTube nature/science animation for kids. Everything we learned making **Episode 1** lives here so every future episode follows the same process and the same quality bar. Read the [[#Prime directives]] first, then jump to the section you need.

## Prime directives

> [!important] The five rules that override everything
> 1. **Consistency is the brand.** Bentley and Pip must look identical in every shot — see [[Bentley Master Prop]] and [[Pip the Hermit Crab]].
> 2. **Reuse before you generate.** Spend credits only when nothing approved fits — see [[Token Efficiency Rules]].
> 3. **Everything is alive.** No static water, trees, grass, clouds, or skies — see [[Animation Rules]].
> 4. **Entertain first, teach second.** One real, verified "wow" fact per episode — see [[Storytelling Rules]] and [[National Geographic Standards]].
> 5. **QA gates everything.** Nothing ships until it passes the [[Episode QA Checklist]].
