# 🦀 Pip — Master Prop

> [!important]
> Pip is the Episode 1 guest. Like [[Bentley Master Prop|Bentley]], he is generated by editing **from his approved master** and QA'd against these rules before any motion/talking render. Early on he rendered as "3–4 different crabs" — that inconsistency is exactly the bug this note exists to prevent.

## Who he is
A small, worried, soft-hearted **hermit crab** who is scared of growing up — and terrified of the open surf. His arc teaches the **vacancy chain** in [[Episode 1 - The Voice in the Shell]].

## ✅ Must have
| Feature | Spec |
|---|---|
| Body | **Reddish-orange** |
| Eyes | **Big, soulful**, set **IN his face** |
| Shell | **Dull, cracked, brown SPIRAL** shell |
| Face | **Clean** |
| Mouth | Small **natural crab mouth** |
| Surface | **Smooth** shell |

## ⛔ Must never have
| Never | Why |
|---|---|
| **Eye-stalks** | He has soulful in-face eyes, not stalks |
| **White / nautilus shell** | His shell is a dull brown **spiral** |
| **Antennae or whiskers** | Clean face |
| **Human teeth** | Natural crab mouth only |
| **Fur on the shell** | The "shell with hairs" artifact — see [[Lessons Learned]] |

## Production note
> [!warning] Pip + moving water
> Never put Pip in a **talking/Avatar** shot when a wave or live water must move behind him — the Avatar model **freezes the background**. Split the beat into a **live-water motion shot** + a **dry talking close-up**. This is the lesson that cost 5 failed "frozen wave" attempts — see [[Animation Rules]] and [[Lessons Learned]].
