# 🐶 Bentley — Master Prop (Single Source of Truth)

> [!important] What "master prop" means
> Every shot of Bentley is generated by **editing FROM the approved master image**, never from a blank prompt. The master locks her design. If a render disagrees with this note, **the render is wrong** — re-roll it. QA every Bentley render against the tables below *before* spending credits on a motion or talking version.

## Who she is
A female **red-fawn Boxer puppy**, based on the owner's real dog. Curious, kind, brave, expressive. She is the **star** — in any episode, the last face the audience sees should be hers.

## ✅ Must have
| Feature | Spec |
|---|---|
| Breed / sex | Female **Boxer** puppy; **natural Boxer proportions** (broad head, short muzzle, compact sturdy body) |
| **Tail** | **Short docked stub** |
| Face | **Black mask**, **grey chin**; consistent **face shape & muzzle** every time |
| Markings | **White chest blaze**; rich **red-fawn** coat |
| **Paws** | **Solid red-fawn paws — NO white socks** |
| Ears | Soft, **floppy, folded DOWN** |
| Eyes | **Soulful, well-proportioned** — warm and expressive, not googly |
| Mouth | **Natural dog mouth**; at most small **lower** teeth |
| Collar | **Teal collar** with a glowing **spiral seashell charm** |

## ⛔ Must never have
| Never | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Long or upright tail** | Breaks the breed read — the **#1 recurring error** |
| **Human teeth / wide human grin** | Uncanny; a locked violation that kept appearing in close-ups |
| **Upright / pricked ears** | Wrong silhouette (*unless* a future design intentionally changes it) |
| **Different face or muzzle structure** | Destroys recognizability |
| **Different age appearance** | She is always a **puppy** |
| **Different coat markings** | Markings are identity |
| **White socks / white paws** | Paws are **solid red-fawn** (owner-confirmed); `fix_paws.py` strips any white socks |
| **Fur on shells / props** | A prompt-bleed artifact — see [[Lessons Learned]] |

> [!success] Paws — RESOLVED (owner-confirmed)
> Bentley's paws are **solid red-fawn with NO white socks**. An earlier draft spec briefly listed "white paws" — that was a slip; the owner confirmed **Episode 1's look is canonical**. `fix_paws.py` / `paw_fix()` correctly **remove** any white socks a render adds — keep that behavior.

## Master-prop workflow
1. **Start from the master.** Generate Bentley with `nano-banana-edit`, passing the master URL as the reference image — never a blank text-to-image.
2. **Bake the rules into the prompt.** Always include: short docked tail · ears floppy down · no human teeth · soulful eyes · teal collar + spiral charm · **bright** lighting.
3. **Keyframe-QA first.** View the still and check it against the tables above **before** the expensive motion/talking render. Re-roll the *keyframe*, not the clip.
4. **Lighting reality:** Nano-Banana reads "golden dawn" as *moody dusk*, and the Kling Avatar **darkens** faces — push **bright / high-key** in the prompt and lift in the grade. See [[Animation Rules]] and [[Lessons Learned]].
